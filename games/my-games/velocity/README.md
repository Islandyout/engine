# VELOCITY: How to Play

You are Velocity, an original speedster, keeping Meridian Ward safe: a daylight city district of avenues, a market street, a park, a construction site and a transit plaza. Open **VELOCITY** from the editor's Games library, or its page on the published site.

## Controls

| Action | Keyboard / mouse | Gamepad |
|---|---|---|
| Move | W A S D | left stick |
| Look | mouse (drag) | right stick |
| Sprint | hold Shift (in every direction) | left stick click |
| Jump | Space | A |
| Light combo | left click or J | X |
| Heavy | right click or K | Y |
| Kick | L | B |
| Kinetic Burst (and later specials) | U (with forward, back, or back-forward) | RB |
| Dodge | X or Alt | LT |
| Block (tap as a blow lands to parry) | R | LB |
| Lock on / switch target | Tab or middle click | right stick click |
| Use (rescue, inspect, start a mission) | G | d-pad up |
| Map | M | |
| Back to the checkpoint | Backspace | Back |
| Pause and settings | Esc | |

Every key can be rebound in the settings (Esc).

## The campaign: Momentum: First Response

1. **First Response** (the tutorial). Sprint to Market Row, jump the barrier, stop a Street Enforcer, help a civilian. It unlocks the Kinetic Burst.
2. **Streetlight**. A robbery on Market Row: two enforcers, then a Shield Heavy, then the device they dropped. It unlocks the Kinetic Lunge (forward + U) and the first mastery tier.
3. **No Brakes**. A runaway delivery rig with stolen kinetic cells. Catch it twice on the move, then disable it at the roadblock. Fall behind and it slows; it never gets away.
4. **Aftershock**. Get three workers out of Construction Rise, in any order, past the threat near each one. It unlocks Rising Strike (back + U) and Meteor Smash (back, forward + U).
5. **Breakline**. The finale at Meridian Junction, in three phases:
   - Phase 1: a two-hit chain and a red shoulder charge. Dodge the charge.
   - Phase 2 (below 70%): adds a red slam ring. Get clear or roll through it.
   - Phase 3 (below 35%): after each slam, an impact field soaks hits for three seconds. Wait it out, then punish.

Each mission after the first starts at its marker: walk in and press G.

## Street incidents

Five short incidents sit on the map (orange markers): Robbery in Progress, Medical Dash, Runaway Rig, Device Shutdown and Roofline Relay (timed gates for gold, silver or bronze). Each pays merit, rests a minute, then can be played again. None is needed for the story.

## Mastery

Each of Missions 1 to 3 opens a tier at **Ward Dispatch** on the Civic Plaza. Walk up, press G, and choose with the number keys. Changes are free.

| Tier | Choice | Choice |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Swift: attacks and dodges 10% quicker | Heavy Hands: +15% damage |
| 2 | Steady: +25% max health | Edge: 12% critical hits |
| 3 | Battery: start each fight with half an energy bar | Second Wind: recover 25% health when a fight ends |

After the finale, Dispatch also turns **Heroic** on and off (H): enemies hit harder and last longer.

## Failing is cheap

Knocked down (below 12% health), you are back on your feet at the last checkpoint, and the encounter starts over. Backspace does the same at any time. Missed timers in incidents just end the attempt.

## Settings

Esc pauses the game and opens the settings:
- volume;
- look sensitivity and invert Y;
- camera shake and reduced motion;
- brightness and impact flashes;
- the key list.

## Saving

Progress saves in the browser as you go: missions done, merit, mastery picks and Heroic.

## Known limitations

- Characters are the engine's tinted mannequin: no bespoke hero or villain models yet.
- There is no wall-running. Speed is ground sprinting, jumping and the specials.
- Mastery uses the stats the engine exposes (damage, speed, crit, health, energy). The design's sprint-ramp and air-steering choices aren't in yet, because scripts can't change the character controller.
- No difficulty below the default. Heroic is the harder preset; there is no separate Challenge preset.
- Cosmetic rewards (suit colours, trails) aren't in yet.
