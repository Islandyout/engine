# F120 Eden: culture and body, finished

**Culture**
- A fourth custom forms by itself: **gathering at the fire at dusk**. When people are together at a burning fire around sunset, it counts; once it is a custom they go there at dusk, and it eases loneliness and lifts the mood.
- **Shunning**: someone the others have lost faith in (average trust under 0.22, in a group of three or more) is shunned: not sought out, not given food unless they are kin, noted in the chronicle and the others' journals. It ends when trust comes back above 0.35.
- **Stories**: someone tells another a thing that mattered a long time ago (kills, births, losses), more often the older they are, never twice to the same listener; the listener writes it in their journal as told to them.

**Body**
- **Infants** (under one year) do not choose anything: they stay by their mother, are nursed (her hunger rises, theirs and their thirst fall), and without a mother they starve.
- **Hygiene**: people get dirty over about four days and go to the water to wash; the dirty fall ill more easily.
- **Diet**: eating only one kind of food (fruit, grain, stored) for a couple of days is a deficiency that makes illness likelier.
- **Remedies**: a skilled healer (healing 45% or more) gives herbs that ease the sick faster.

The childbirth test now tries twelve couples and wants at least six births (birth is a chance); an infant that was never given water had died of thirst in a few days, which the old test hid and the new one found.
