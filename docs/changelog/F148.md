# F148 Eden phase K: seen in the running game, a browser test, performance, one design document

**Seen in the running game (K1).** Headless Chromium (software GL) drove the built `eden.html`; screenshots are in `docs/eden-screenshots/`.

Seen: the rowboat and pier at the lake (`lake_*.png`, `sky_b.png`); birds in the sky (`sky_b.png`, `screen_journal.png`, `screen_pack.png`,
`minds_tab.png`); the header reading "WINTER · -2°C · SNOW" with flakes falling (`snow_4.png`); the people form in the Creator tab and the
person it made (`people_form_filled.png`, `people_form_made.png`); the journal, pack and map screens (`screen_*.png`); the talk panel with its
mic button and quick phrases (`talk_with_mic.png`); the Minds tab (`minds_tab.png`).

Not seen: a fish leaping (six frames at the lake, none caught one); snow lying on the ground (the header and flakes showed snow, but the
ground did not look white in the shot); belly, dirt or a limp (the people were patched with `edenMind` but Eve was out of frame and Adam's
limp was not visible); a plan or a party (no plan was on screen in the Minds tab); the pad buttons (no gamepad in headless); the planner
setting (the Voice box was closed in the shot); music (headless has no audio check). Also in the shots: the boat and pier look to sit well
above the water line in `minds_tab.png`; not investigated.

**Bug found and fixed.** The activity line read "Adam undefined the lake" while someone washed: `VERB` in `edenAgents.ts` had no entries for
the `wash` and `sew` intents. Both added ("goes to wash at", "sits down to sew a warmer cloak"). No simulation code changed.

**A browser test (K3).** `tests/browser/eden.cjs` loads Eden, waits for Adam and Eve and their bodies, runs at 30x, checks someone moved in the
mind and on screen, that the header shows season and time, that the people form makes a person, and that no console error or uncaught
exception happened. It is in the editor workflow (`.github/workflows/editor.yml`) after `city.cjs`; no new action was added, so nothing new
to pin. It passed locally (Adam 2.7 m, Eve 0.4 m in 40 s on a busy machine; the thresholds are 1 m).
