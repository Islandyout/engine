# F93 — Empty hands until a pickup; one item in hand

- **A Player starts empty-handed** (editor and Play). `Weapons.loadout` is what a Player *carries*: 1-9, Q, the wheel or a pickup draws a slot; until then the fire button does nothing and no gun or blade is drawn. Soldiers and other NPCs still hold their loadout's first slot.
- **No default rifle**: a character with `Melee` or a third-person `CharacterController` but no loadout (the rifle appeared in its hands) now holds nothing. `CharacterController` only changes movement and camera; it never decides what is in the hands.
- **A Player's `Melee.rightHand` / `leftHand` blades are no longer drawn at start** (an NPC fighter's still are): a Player fights bare-handed until it picks a blade up.
- **Nobody holds a gun and a blade at once**: `offhand=` is only valid on a melee slot (sword + shield); a gun with `offhand=` is rejected with a reason. A fighter NPC's `Melee` hand blades are hidden while a gun is in hand.
- C++: `Arsenal.holstered` (set for a Player's loadout), `editor_weapon_value(i, 10)` = 1 with a weapon in hand. Pickups draw the picked weapon.
- Tests: bridge tests for the empty-handed start and the `offhand=` rule; existing Player-with-loadout tests/browser tests draw slot 1 first.
