# F71 — Spaceflight: star systems, a flown ship, and the Pale Signal slice (0.71.0)

Built so the engine can carry [Pale Signal](https://github.com/Islandyout/pale-signal): manual, seamless flight from a planet's surface to orbit and down on another world, plus on-foot exploration at a site. The slice that proves it is documented in [docs/space/PALE_SIGNAL_SLICE.md](space/PALE_SIGNAL_SLICE.md).

## `engine::gameplay::space` (include/engine/gameplay/space.hpp)

- **Double-precision math**: `DVec3` and `DQuat`, with `rotate`, `between`, `axis_angle` and `from_axes`.
- **`System` and `Body`**: circular orbits around the star or a parent (period, phase, inclination), radius, surface gravity, and an exponential atmosphere. Air thins with a scale height of 0.42× `atmosphere_height` and ends at 2.4×.
  - **Terrain**: deterministic 3D value-noise fBm with ridged mountains (`terrain_amplitude`, `terrain_scale`, `seed`).
  - **`Flat`**: levels the ground around a direction, for sites and pads.
  - **Queries**: `body_position`, `body_velocity`, `air_density`, `surface_height` / `_radius` / `_normal`.
- **Spheres of influence**: a tenth of the orbit around the star, a third around a planet, always wide enough to enclose a planet's moons.
- **`step_ship`** (1/120 s substeps):
  - **Attitude**: rate-limited and pilot-first. Releasing an axis stops it.
  - **Assists**:
    - `manual`
    - `stabilized`: levels to the horizon below 4 km or the atmosphere top; in space it stops rotation. Under 3 km the stick commands a climb or sink rate that eases off near the ground.
    - `prograde`, `retrograde` and `target`: point the nose.
  - **Thrust**: main engine along the nose, belly thrusters along the ship's up, with fuel burn for each.
  - **Atmosphere**: drag; air turning the velocity toward the nose; heating that damages the hull above a tolerance.
  - **Landing**:
    - Lift-off happens once thrust beats weight.
    - Touchdown only while descending.
    - Clean or rough is judged on sink rate, drift, tilt and slope, with damage scaled to the excess.
    - An anti-tunnelling guard stops fast ships passing through the ground.
  - **Handover**: reference bodies change at sphere-of-influence boundaries.
  - **Surface**: a `SurfaceQuery` lets the host raise the ground for structures.
- **Placement and readouts**: `place_landed` and `place_in_orbit`; `orbit_elements` (periapsis, apoapsis, eccentricity, semi-major axis, period); `predict_path`.
- **Tests**: `tests/space_tests.cpp` (see the slice document's test plan).

## Runtime bridge (`SpaceSim`, `Spaceship`)

- **Staging**:
  - `editor_space_begin`: star, site body, latitude/longitude, flat radius, walkable range, start time.
  - `editor_space_body`: one call per body.
  - `editor_set_spaceship`: tuning, start in the seat, start landed or in orbit.
  - **At commit**:
    - The site's flat is added and the site frame is built, with +y along the local vertical.
    - The walkable heightfield is sampled from the body's surface in site coordinates (curvature included) and becomes the physics terrain.
    - The ship's RigidBody is removed; the space simulation owns its motion.
    - The ship is placed landed where it was authored, resting on any pad collider under it, or in orbit.
- **`editor.space`** (order 5, before physics) and **`editor.space_after`** (order 14):
  - **Pilot input**: W/S, Shift and X throttle; arrows, IJKL and A/D attitude; Q/E roll; Space/C belly thrust; T and N assists; 9/0 warp (coasting only); E to leave when landed or board within reach.
  - **Surface**: the planet, raised to a site collider just below the ship.
  - **Each tick**: the ship's Box follows it; the stowed pilot is a kinematic trigger that rides along; on foot, the player is held inside the walkable range.
  - **Events**: `touchdown`, `rough_touchdown`, `liftoff`, `soi:<body>`, `destroyed`, `boarded`, `exited`, `exit_blocked`.
- **Readouts**: `editor_space_value` (pose in the site frame, flight readouts, orbit, warp, assist), `editor_space_body_value`, `editor_space_frame` (body→site rotation), `editor_planet_height`, `editor_space_ground`, `editor_space_path` / `_value`.
- **Lua `space.*`**: `state` (a table, including latitude/longitude over the reference body), `events`, `set_warp`, `set_assist`, `set_target`, `refuel`, `set_fuel`, `repair`, `board`, `exit`, `set_controls`, `place_landed`, `place_orbit`, `body`.

## Editor

- **Components**:
  - `SpaceSystem`: bodies as text lines with colours, star GM and colour, site, walkable range, start time, landmarks.
  - `Spaceship`: mass, thrusts, turn authority and rate, fuel and burn, hull, gear clearance, start in the seat, start in orbit.
- **`spaceView.ts`**, during Play:
  - **Planets**:
    - Each planet is a cube-sphere quadtree of 17×17 chunks with skirts, meshed from `editor_planet_height`.
    - Chunks are coloured by height and slope.
    - Detail streams in coarse first within a per-frame build budget.
    - Each mesh sits at its chunk's centre, so far-away geometry stays precise.
  - **The system pass**: rendered first with its own camera. The near plane scales with height above the terrain and the far plane is 1e8 m. The scene renders over it with its depth cleared.
  - **Sky and space**: atmosphere rim shells seen from outside; an opaque sky dome blending the body's haze colour by air and daylight; stars; a sun disc; fog in thick air.
  - **Flight aids**: landmark beams that fade as you arrive; the predicted path in the reference body's frame.
  - **The ship**: interpolated between ticks; engine flame, belly glow, re-entry plasma and landing dust; a thruster sound filtered by air density.
  - **Chase camera**: follows the ship's attitude with a lag, and drag looks around without steering.
- **Scene integration**:
  - The site's ground is a shadow-catching depth surface, so hills hide what's behind them.
  - The sun, sky light and fog come from the system, with no sun or shadows below the horizon.
  - The grid is hidden.
- **Flight HUD**: assist and warp, reference body, altitude, vertical, ground and orbital speed, periapsis/apoapsis, throttle, fuel, hull, heat, landing guidance under 150 m, target and landmark markers with distance, other bodies from space, and prompts. A text copy goes to `#hud-text`.

## The slice

`tools/space/build_pale_signal.ts` builds the scene:
- **The system**: Tethys, Vell, Cinder and Ossuary; Kestra Station at 30°N 40°E on Tethys.
- **The ship and station**: the Kestrel, built from primitive parts; a landing pad, a hangar and an office.
- **The world around it**: a Talari quarter with inhabitants, the ruin, three volatile ice outcrops, forest, boulders and wildlife.
- **Interface**: UI and a director.

`tools/space/lua/director.lua` runs the beats (see the slice document). Tests: `apps/editor/tests/paleSignal.test.ts` and `tests/browser/pale_signal.cjs` (in CI).
