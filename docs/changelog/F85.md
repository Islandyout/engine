# F85 — VELOCITY: Momentum: First Response (0.85.0)

VELOCITY ([GAME_DESIGN.md](../../games/my-games/velocity/GAME_DESIGN.md)) grows from a one-street sandbox into a complete vertical slice. How to play: [README](../../games/my-games/velocity/README.md).

The scene is still `games/my-games/velocity/velocity.json`. It is now generated by `tools/velocity/build_velocity.ts` (`npm run velocity` in `apps/editor`) with `ward.ts`, plus the Lua in `tools/velocity/lua/`.

## Meridian Ward

- A daylight district of about 208 × 260 m:
  - Civic Plaza (the start, and Ward Dispatch);
  - Central Avenue and two side avenues;
  - Market Row;
  - Meridian Green;
  - the Service Alley;
  - Construction Rise, with a ramp and a scaffold deck;
  - the Transit Plaza;
  - Meridian Junction.
- Everything static is instanced, so the district is 50 entities.
- A minimap and map (M) show areas, roads, the next mission and the incidents.

## Velocity

- **Movement**: walks about 9.5 m/s and sprints 2.5× that in every direction, with a light trail and sparks at speed and a km/h readout.
- **Combat** uses the engine's martial-arts kit:
  - light combo, heavy, kick, dodge, block and parry, lock-on;
  - the specials unlock with the story: Kinetic Burst, Kinetic Lunge, Rising Strike, Meteor Smash.

## The campaign

Five missions, all completable solo:
1. **First Response**: the tutorial; sprint, jump a barrier, fight, rescue.
2. **Streetlight**: two enforcers, a Shield Heavy, a dropped device.
3. **No Brakes**: an AI-driven runaway rig, caught twice on the move, then disabled at a roadblock. It slows when Velocity falls behind, never escapes.
4. **Aftershock**: three workers rescued in any order, each with a threat nearby.
5. **Breakline**: the boss, in three phases announced once each at 70% and 35%:
   - a telegraphed charge;
   - a slam ring;
   - an impact field that soaks hits for three seconds after each slam.

Each later mission starts at its marker (G).

## The rest

- **Enemy roster**:
  - Street Enforcer (martial arts);
  - Rusher (a red, dodge-only shoulder charge);
  - Suppressor (ranged bursts);
  - Shield Heavy (guards its front, red guard-breaking slam).
- **Five street incidents**: Robbery in Progress, Medical Dash, Runaway Rig, Device Shutdown and Roofline Relay (timed, with medals). Each pays merit, rests a minute, can be replayed, and never gates the story.
- **Mastery at Ward Dispatch**: three tiers of two sidegrades each, opened by Missions 1–3, changeable for free. After the finale, Dispatch also toggles **Heroic** (enemies +30% damage and health).
- **Failure**: knocked down below 12% health, Velocity is back at the step's checkpoint and the encounter respawns. Backspace does the same at any time.
- **Save** (`velocity.save`): `1|missions|merit|picks`. Rewards are granted once.
