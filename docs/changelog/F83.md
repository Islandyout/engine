# F83 — GATEBREAKER M3: shadows, the C–B Gates, gear, loot and the city (0.83.0)

GATEBREAKER's third milestone ([GAME_DESIGN.md §5.4, §12, §13](../gatebreaker/GAME_DESIGN.md)) and the first steps of M3.5 (items) and M4 (the world). The scene is still `examples/gatebreaker/gatebreaker.json`, built by `tools/gatebreaker/build_game.ts` (plus `district.ts` and `lua/world.lua`).

## Shadows

- **Binding**: an elite or boss that falls leaves black-violet smoke for 8 s. Stand in it and **hold G** for a second: **ARISE**. Elites always rise; a Gate master gets 3 tries at odds shown on the prompt (50% a try at SEN 10, better with SEN). A shadow you already have never asks again.
- **Three roles**, one shadow each: a **tank** (Shieldbearer, Brute, Armored Knight), a **striker** (Hobgoblin, Chieftain, Warlord, the new bosses) and an **archer** (Shaman, Cultist Caster). A new shadow of a role replaces the old one.
- **No commands**: shadows walk at your side (`melee.follow`), fight what's near you and never chase past 16 m. When you use **Thousand Fangs** they use their own big move, and when an enemy **Breaks** every shadow steps to it and strikes.
- They rise at each Gate's door, follow you into sealed rooms, and the fallen rise again when a room is cleared. The Ledger saves them; the status window (C) lists them.
- **Clear grade** S/A/B/C from time and damage taken. A better grade pays up to 50% more of the Gate's reward, and the reward window shows the S target.

## Two new Gates

- **Flooded Temple** (C): standing water, teal torches. **Armored Knights**, which wear visible armour and teach parrying, **Cultist Casters**, which shoot from range, Drowned goblins, and the **Drowned Priest**, who calls the drowned in phase 2.
- **Ice Fortress** (B, the rank test): snow, cold light, fast **Ice Ghouls**, knights and the **Frost Knight Commander**.
- Rank-ups to **C** and **B** with their own ceremonies; the Gate Board lists all 5 Gates and says what each locked one needs; levels go to 15; new story quests.
- There are no spiders: no CC0 spider model on the combat skeleton exists. Ice Ghouls take their place.

## Items and gear

- **Gear you can see**: body armour, bracers, trousers and boots (CC0 Quaternius outfits on the hunter's own skeleton, `tools/models/make_outfits.mjs`). New Lua: `world.wear("214 215:#8aa6d6 …")` dresses a character, each piece optionally tinted.
- **Loot**: kills drop gear and potions as a beam in the rarity's colour (Common, Rare, Epic, Legendary). Walk over it to pick it up; a feed bottom right names it in its colour; the Ledger gathers what you left when you leave the Gate. Gate masters drop Epic or better a third of the time, and the fifth in a row without one always does.
- **The bag (I)**: 40 slots, sorted by slot and rarity. Pick an item to see exactly what wearing it changes; Enter wears it, X sells it, S sells every Common, W changes the look.
- **Six slots** (body, bracers, trousers, boots, ring, necklace) with a main stat and extra stats by rarity, and a **set** per Gate with 2- and 4-piece bonuses.
- **Smith Kang** upgrades worn gear +1…+10 (no failure chance; upgrades move to a better piece when you swap) and sells potions.
- **Potions**: 1 heals 40% of max health, 2 restores 50 mana, 8 s apart. New Lua: `melee.mana(add?)`.
- **Wardrobe**: each Gate's set colours and Shadow black, worn over any gear.

## The city

- A **district of Seoul** around the Association (`tools/gatebreaker/district.ts`): streets, a park, the Hangang riverside plaza, apartment and office blocks, landmark towers, a bus stop and parked traffic.
- **Gate sites**: three cordoned squares; a marker shows the nearest open one, and they rotate with the day.
- **Subway**: G at Station A or B rides to the other.
- **15 new CC0 vehicles** (`tools/models/import_vehicles.mjs`): family sedan, compact wagon, coupe, GT, crossover SUV, sedan taxi, roadster, limousine, panel van, step van, ambulance van, fire truck, coach bus and two bicycles, each baked to real size (a sedan 4.7 m, the coach 12 m).

## Look

- **Gates read as panels**: dim, cold overhead light so torches make warm pools; more contrast and vignette; a closer camera.
- **Lighter toon shading**: softer bands with some of the smooth light kept, fewer interior ink lines.
- **Lit streets, dark Gates**: new Lua `fx.light(ambient, sun)` sets the light over the Environment's, so one scene holds both.
- **The genre research** behind all this, and the design critic that checks features against player reviews, are in GAME_DESIGN.md §12–13 and `.claude/agents/design-critic.md`.

## Engine and editor

- **Instanced models are drawn in play**: a `ModelInstances` entity with no `Renderable` was hidden as a helper in Play (the racing city's roads too). They stay out of the simulation and are drawn.
- **Gate geometry is instanced**: one entity per Gate and height instead of one per wall and tile, so five Gates and a district fit under the 1024-entity cap (314 entities).
- `ui.set_color(name, "#rrggbb")` colours a UI element's text.
- **Specular highlights are capped** under the Manhwa style, so edge-on leaves no longer bloom into white blotches.
