# F125 Eden: people move like people

The villager models only carry idle, talk, walk, run, wave, hit and death, so a person eating, drinking, resting or sleeping stood playing "idle". `editor/poses.ts` now poses the skeleton over the clip, eased in and out:

- sit (rest), eat (hand to mouth, a bite rhythm), drink and wash (a crouch with the hands down), forage/gather/plant/stash (bent to reach), work (build, craft, sew, fire), fish, mourn (seated, head bowed), warm (hands out to the fire), sleep (lying on the back on the ground).
- Rotations are made about the figure's own axes, so they do not depend on how each bone was exported; the legs' shoe control bones are carried with the shins; the foot-on-slope adjustment is off while posed.
- Seen in a headless browser: sit, eat and sleep look right. Drink, forage, work, fish, mourn and warm are not yet looked at (screenshots on a software renderer take a minute each).
- Debug hooks (BTAIDesktop only): `edenPose(name, pose, yaw)`, `edenBones(name)`.
