# F122 Eden: tools, a livelier valley, and the people notice you

**Tools (design phase 9)**
- **What if**: three places to keep the world as it is (Creator tab: Keep 1/2/3) and to go back to (Back to 1/2/3, showing the day kept), so you can try a drought or a new arrival and return.
- **Bonds**: the Chronicle tab draws who is to whom as a graph: friends green, those at odds red, partners gold, thicker where there is more trust.
- **Life story**: the Minds tab lists each person's big moments (everything that mattered most), above their journal.

**The valley**
- New animals: stags (big deer, prey), milk cows (a little to drink and eat from each day, never killed) and donkeys. More plants: blossom trees in the old orchard, aspens along the river, rough grass in the highlands, bushes in the pines and woods.
- Elders (55 and over) have their own slightly shorter bodies (nine looks are now four sizes: infant-child, youth, adult, elder).

**You and the people**
- The people notice the Walker: the first time you come within 14 m they write it down, wave a hello and are glad of the company; if you bring down game near hungry people they are fed and grateful.
