# F131 Eden: the Walker's screens, voice input, pad buttons, people form, tutorial

**Screens.** M opens a map (places by name, rivers, shelters, graves, creatures, every person by name, you as a blue arrow), L a journal (what you have done, plus the chronicle of the land), I your pack and who knows you (trust bar, what they ask for). Same key or Esc closes. The journal is saved with the world (`world.walker.notes`).

**Tutorial.** The first time you walk, a card lists the keys. It is remembered per browser.

**Voice input.** A mic button in the talk box uses the browser's speech recognition and fills the text field. Where the browser has none (or it fails) the box says so and you type.

**Pad.** A = talk, X = gather, Y = hand over, Back = map, Start = journal. Walking itself is the engine's pad support.

**People form.** The Creator tab makes a person from a name, kind (woman, man, girl, boy) and a tilt of character; it sends the same commands as typing.

**Long run.** Three 12-year worlds: all stayed at the population cap with births, partners, teaching and grief, no collapse. Not built yet: inheritance of goods, settlements beyond the garden.
