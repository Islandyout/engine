# F86 — Realistic assets, foliage rules, living streets and the City Template (0.86.0)

The engine's towns now look and behave like real ones. The toy-like kit trees, vehicles and animals are gone. A rule-based foliage system decides where plants grow. Junctions have lights that traffic obeys, and people walk the sidewalks and cross with the lights. A **City Template** ties it all together for future games ([how it works](../world/CITY_TEMPLATE.md)).

## Assets: realistic replaces toy-like

- **Retired:**
  - the Aether kit's trees (ids 45–53), vehicles (91–104) and animals (105–118);
  - their files are deleted.
- **New:**
  - **Trees**: oak, ash, aspen, a young street tree, pine, small pine and a leafy bush (ids 255–261). They are made with ez-tree by `tools/models/make_trees.mjs`, at real heights, each with a far LOD.
  - **Animals**: cow, bull, deer, donkey, fox, horse, white horse and a Shiba Inu (236–243), from Quaternius' Ultimate Animated Animals.
  - **Pedestrians**: seven men and four women (244–254), from Quaternius' Ultimate Modular Men and Animated Women packs.
  - **Vehicles**: a pickup and a police car (234–235), joining the realistic cars already in the catalog.
- `tools/models/import_creatures.mjs`:
  - keeps only the clips the editor plays (idle, walk, run, graze, talk, sit);
  - bakes each rig's rest pose and real size into its vertices, so tools and the editor read true bounds.
- **Older scenes still load:** `catalogReplacements` draws each retired id's replacement and says so in the log. The four birds have no replacement and stay boxes.
- **Every game is migrated** to the new ids and real sizes:
  - VELOCITY, GATEBREAKER, Pale Signal, Last Signal and High Heat;
  - the demo scene and the editor's templates.
- **Trees collide at their trunk:**
  - `trunk` in ModelInstances and scatter rules gives a post a twentieth of the tree's height across (0.3–1.2 m);
  - before, a tree blocked a box the size of its canopy.
- Sources, licences and hashes: `assets/CREDITS.md`. The ez-tree MIT notice is `third_party/ez-tree/LICENSE`.

## Foliage placement (`foliage.ts`)

Scatter rules take options:
- **Spacing** (`spacing=`): Poisson-disk thinning across all rules.
- **Groves with clearings** (`cluster=`, `clusterSize=`): noise-driven.
- **Altitude bands** (`height=`).
- **Ground layers** (`on=grass,sand,rock,snow`).
- **Lean with the slope** (`tilt=`).
- **Per-instance brightness** (`tint=`, drawn with instance colours).
- **Circular exclusions**: `exclude x z r`.

A rule with no options places exactly as before. Instanced models with a far version (a `LOD1` node) switch to it per chunk past 60 m.

## Streets (`engine/gameplay/street.hpp`)

- **TrafficSignal**: a junction's light cycle (green, yellow and all red per road, with an offset for green waves).
  - Traffic-mode drivers plan a gentle stop at the line on red.
  - They carry on through a yellow they can't stop for.
  - They brake for people on foot ahead.
  - The editor draws the signal heads, lit from the simulation.
- **Walker**: someone on foot following a sidewalk route.
  - `x,z*` marks a kerb to wait at: for the walk light (green plus clearance covering the crossing), or for a gap in the traffic at an unsignalised crossing.
  - `follow` keeps a dog a lead's length behind its owner.

## The City Template

`tools/city/city.ts` (`buildCity`) and `npm run city` build a 3 × 3-block town:
- 16 signalised junctions with a green wave;
- zoned perimeter blocks: downtown, mixed, residential, industrial and a park;
- street furniture by NACTO's sidewalk zones;
- courtyard parking;
- a park of groves and clearings with foxes and a deer;
- 14 cars looping blocks in the right lane;
- 36 people on the sidewalks, five walking dogs.

It opens from the Games library. The research behind it (techniques, planning guides, and what players say on GTAForums, Steam, ResetEra and in reviews) and every number it uses are in [CITY_TEMPLATE.md](../world/CITY_TEMPLATE.md).
