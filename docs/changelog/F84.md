# F84 — GATEBREAKER M4: the world, E to S (0.84.0)

GATEBREAKER's fourth milestone ([GAME_DESIGN.md §13](../gatebreaker/GAME_DESIGN.md)): the district becomes a place to play in, and progression runs all the way from E to S. The scene is still `examples/gatebreaker/gatebreaker.json`, built by `tools/gatebreaker/build_game.ts` with `district.ts` and the Lua in `tools/gatebreaker/lua/`.

## The field

- **Dungeon breaks**: three zones in the district (the west avenue, the riverside, Midtown east) each spill a pack of 3–4 enemies matched to the hunter's level. Packs patrol their zone and fight within a leash.
- **Respawn out of sight**: a fallen pack returns after 90–120 s, but only when the hunter is at least 40 m away, out of a fight and looking elsewhere. No zone holds more than one pack, and no more than 12 field enemies stand at once.
- **Field kills pay** XP, gold and loot through the Ledger, as in a Gate, and save at once.
- **A daily field boss** waits at its marker until killed, and returns the next day.
- **Night**: field enemies have 1.3x health and damage, and drop gear half again as often, with better rarity odds.
- **Gates in the world**: each open Gate site holds a rift in its rank's colour. Walk in, press G, and you enter that Gate. The Gate Board in the hub still works.
- Below 25% health in the field, the hunter is pulled back to the square and healed.

## Finding your way

- **Minimap** (top right): small, medium or large (a setting). It turns with the camera, with N on the rim, and shows Gate sites, stations, shops, quest givers and the tracked quest.
- **M** opens the district map, with a legend and every marker. N toggles north-up; M, Esc or a click closes it.
- **J** opens the quest log: the story quest, the Daily Quest and side quests, each with its objective and reward. Keys 1–5 track one; the HUD tracker shows it in up to 3 lines.
- **Quest givers**, with a ! over their heads: Officer Yoon (a daily dungeon-break patrol), Courier Bae (a parcel to Hangang Station) and Apprentice Jin (3 Rare items for an Epic).

## Day and night

- **A 24-minute day**: day runs 06:00–22:00 and night 22:00–06:00, with dawn and dusk blends. The HUD shows "Day N · HH:MM". Resting at home brings 06:00.
- **Night stays easy to see**: a moonlit blue-violet, with ambient light above 60% of the day's and lit streetlamps. Inside a Gate, the Gate's own light.
- **Smith Kang** upgrades by day only; potions sell at all hours. At night his sign says when he opens and that resting skips to morning.

## Comfort

- **Esc settings** (also when the captured mouse is released): master, music and effects volume, brightness, quality, ink lines, impact flashes, camera shake, look sensitivity, invert Y, hold or toggle block, minimap size, and the key list with rebinding. The game pauses while it is open.
- **Sprint**: hold Space out of a fight (no lock-on, no enemy within 12 m) to run at 8.5 m/s. A tap still dodges, and in a fight Space dodges on the press.
- J and K no longer attack (J is the quest log). The smith's mana potion moved from M to O, so M always opens the map.

## The A and S Gates

- **The Bloodstone Citadel** (A, the A-rank test): Castle Imps, Bloodstone Knights and Blood Mages that blink away when you close in. Its master, the **Crimson Castellan**, adds the Crimson Rend in phase 2: three red cuts in a row that can't be parried. Dodge them.
- **The Eclipse Spire** (S, the S-rank test): Hollows, Eclipse Wardens and a second wave. Its master, the **Eclipse Herald**, seals itself behind two Wardens in phase 2 (they must die first) while sigils burn where you stand.
- Ranks **B → A** and **A → S** with their ceremonies. The A Gate needs Lv.15 and rank B; the S Gate needs Lv.20 and rank A.
- **Levels run to 25**, each level costing 300 XP more than the last.
- A gear set and a richer loot table for each new Gate. The knights, wardens, mages and both masters can be bound as shadows.
- **Clear grades use a par time per Gate**: S under 75% of par, A under par.

## Engine and editor

- New Lua: `melee.revive()` stands a fallen fighter back up, so field enemies are reused and long sessions never run out of entities. `camera.forward()`, `hud.map_layout`, `hud.map_marker`, `hud.clear_map_marker`, `fx.time_of_day` and `hud.pause_menu`.
- The script watchdog's instruction budget is per call, not per script lifetime.
- The design critic's player research shaped the respawn rules, the minimap, sprint on the dodge key, potions sold at night, the bright night and the par times.

## Tests

- New browser tests: `gatebreaker_field.cjs` (a pack, its loot and a walk-in Gate), `gatebreaker_map.cjs` (minimap, map, quest log, a side quest), `gatebreaker_time.cjs` (night, settings, sprint) and `gatebreaker_high.cjs` (the Bloodstone Citadel to its clear).
