# F124 Eden: the small joys of a day

- `eden/joys.ts`: a catalogue of the small things that make life good (sun on the face, rain's smell, a first drink, firelight, stars, first snow, a shared joke, being alone in peace, a clean face...). A moment is trigger x context (season, hour, sky, company, what they are doing, warmth) x thing x detail x reaction; `countJoys()` gives about 9.5 million distinct moments (a test requires over a million).
- People notice them at their own pace (the curious and patient more often); each lifts joy a little and goes in the journal; the counts show in the tally (`smallJoys`, `joy_<kind>`).
- `docs/EDEN_THINGS.md`: the object categories, how each is built and the order to build them.
