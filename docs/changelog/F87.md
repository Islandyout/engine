# F87 — The editor for people, and third-person shooting (0.87.0)

Two halves. The editor gets easier to use with a mouse, a trackpad or a touch screen, and it gets search. Games get third-person shooting like Fortnite or GTA: characters visibly hold their guns, the camera sits over the shoulder, the crosshair works, and aim assist helps gamepad and touch players. Forty guns and eleven medieval weapons join the catalog. How the shooting works: [SHOOTING.md](../SHOOTING.md).

## Inspector

- **Filter properties**: type to show only the matching components and fields.
- **Expand all / Collapse all.**
- **Cards remember** whether they are open or closed, per component type.
  - The main components (Transform, Renderable, Player, CharacterController, Vehicle, Script, Camera, Environment, Terrain) start open.
  - A card with a problem is always open, and so is a component you just added.
- **Arrow keys**, Home and End move between card headers.
- **Bigger targets**: 32 px buttons, and 44 px on a touch screen.

## Search everywhere

- **Fuzzy matching** (`fuzzy.ts`): exact, prefix, word start, initials, inside a word, then scattered letters (only in short names, not descriptions).
- **Where**:
  - the entity list;
  - a model search across every catalog category (Enter adds the best match);
  - "Find a component to add";
  - the console's commands, each with an example;
  - the Games library.
- **Ctrl/Cmd+K** focuses the search of the panel used last.

## Trackpad and touch

- **Trackpad** (detected, or set in the viewport toolbar):
  - two-finger scroll orbits, Shift + scroll pans;
  - a pinch, or a mouse wheel, zooms.
- **Touch, editing**:
  - tap selects;
  - a long press (or a right-click) opens Frame / Duplicate / Delete;
  - one finger orbits, two pinch and pan;
  - a second finger releases the gizmo, so a pinch over a selection works;
  - the gizmo is bigger on touch.
- **Touch, playing**: any game gets on-screen controls inside the viewport.
  - A thumb stick, a look area and buttons drive a virtual gamepad, so every game's `InputActions` work unchanged.
  - Keys for E, G, Map and Menu.
- `[hidden]` always hides now (a class's display rule overrode it).

## Weapons and animations

- **40 guns** (catalog ids 262–301), from the Quaternius Ultimate Gun Pack, CC0:
  - pistols, revolvers, SMGs, assault rifles, carbines, bullpups, shotguns and sniper rifles;
  - baked by `tools/models/import_weapons.mjs` at real lengths per class, muzzle forward, origin at the firing grip;
  - each with `offhand` and `muzzle` sockets found from the gun's shape (`gun_points.mjs`).
- **11 melee weapons and shields** (302–312), from the Quaternius Medieval Weapons Pack, CC0: axes, hammers, a greatsword, a golden sword, two bows, heater and Celtic shields.
- **The importer checks every source file's SHA-256** against `assets/CREDITS.md` and reproduces the committed files byte for byte.
- **The combat clip library** gains 14 UAL clips (69 in all): pistol idle, aim, shoot and reload, bow stances, and the shield guard, bash and run. The 55 existing clips are unchanged.

## Guns in the hands (`gunHold.ts`)

- **Who**: characters with `Weapons` hold their current gun — a third-person Player and soldiers, on the mannequin and pedestrian rigs.
- **How it is posed**:
  - the stock goes in the shoulder pocket, or a handgun at arm's length;
  - it is lowered to a ready position when not aiming, and carried across the chest when sprinting;
  - two-bone IK puts the hands on the gun's grip and support sockets;
  - the spine turns to the aim.
- **What it shows**: a reload tips the gun and sends the support hand to the magazine; each shot kicks the muzzle.
- **`held=`** names the gun in a loadout line. Without it, a default is chosen for the `model=`.

## Over-the-shoulder aim (`shooterView.ts`)

- **Look**: mouse (with pointer capture), right stick, or the touch look area.
- **Aim** pulls the camera in by the weapon's zoom.
- **V** (or d-pad left) swaps shoulders.
- **The body** faces the aim while the gun is raised.
- **Shots** go from the muzzle to whatever the crosshair ray hits (`editor_set_aim`). Before, a third-person shot used a stale first-person look.
- **The crosshair** is the first-person one, now in third person: its gap follows the spread, it shows hit markers, and it turns red over a hostile.

## Aim assist

- **`engine::gameplay::aim_assist`** gives slowdown, pull and an aim-down-sights snap on a gamepad or touch screen, and a lighter slowdown with a mouse.
- **Only hostile targets** (another team than the Player's) in line of sight count.
- **New Weapons fields** set the strength and reach: `aimAssist` (0..1, default 0.6) and `assistRange` (default 60 m).
- **A player setting** turns it off.
- Native tests: `tests/aim_assist_tests.cpp`, plus the bridge tests.

## Melee loadouts

- **A shield blocks behind the shield**: the UAL shield guard. A sword, a dagger and bare hands keep their own blocks.
- **A dagger and a pistol**: a Player fighter with `Weapons` holds the gun in the free hand.
  - Aiming raises it one-handed and turns the fighter to the aim.
  - Aimed, the fire button shoots; unaimed, it strikes.
  - While aiming, the buttons that aim shares with heavy and block do neither.
