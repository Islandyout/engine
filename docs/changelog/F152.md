# F152: Eden presentation, so the simulation of phases A to K is visible in the running game

Host and scene only (`apps/editor/src/editor/app/edenPresent.ts`, `apps/editor/src/eden/present.ts` for the parts tests can check, `tools/eden/build_eden.ts`, the Lua scripts). Nothing in the simulation's behaviour changed. Screenshots from the running game (headless Chromium, software GL) are in `docs/eden-screenshots/F152_*.png`; the browser test is `tests/browser/eden_present.cjs` (in CI beside `eden.cjs`); unit tests are `apps/editor/tests/edenPresent.test.ts` (16).

## Seen in the running game

- **A bug found first: the water fell.** The lake and river water boxes had their body and collider deleted so people could wade, and with no body at all the runtime let them fall to the terrain: the lake water sat at -3.55 m, on the lake bed, 3 m under the pier and the boat. That is why K's shots showed the boat and pier high and dry (`minds_tab.png`, `lake_*.png`). The water now keeps a fixed body and no collider and sits at -0.4 (`F152_lake_water_fixed.png`, and the water test in `eden_present.cjs` fails on the old scene). The same trap would have dropped the new made-thing prefabs, so they carry a fixed body.
- **I4 faces and blinks** (`F152_face_open/blink/brows_raised/brows_lowered.png`). The models have no lids, so a blink is the eyes taken away for a moment (the skin shows); each person blinks on their own beat, slowly and half-shut when tired, shut in sleep. Brows go up for fear and gladness and down for anger (the brow is one piece: up and down only, no inner tilt for grief, which `faceOf` computes but the model cannot show).
- **I4 lying down to sleep** (`F152_dirt_scars_sleep.png`, Eve lying on the ground at the right). This was already in `poses.ts`; it is now seen.
- **I4 swimming and rowing** (`F152_water_row_swim_bridge.png`): two new whole-body poses, `row` (seated, pulling two oars) and `swim` (face down at the surface). A person on a boat or raft rows and the boat goes with them; one whose boat went over swims. The body is held at the water (`eden_float_<id>` in the Lua) instead of walking the lake bed. Staged with the mind's own state (`tech.afloat` and `route`) set by hand, not by a person choosing to cross.
- **I4 visible wear and dirt** (`F152_dirt_scar_closeup.png`): dirt, and a new `wear` (long filth, lice, age) that fades clothes. A bug in the old code was found: it skipped every mesh named "Eyebrows", and each worn garment is a mesh with that name, so clothes were never tinted; only skin and hair were. Fixed. Dirt on dark clothes is subtle.
- **I3 smooth growth** (`F152_growth_ages.png`: ages 0.4, 1.2, 2.5, 3.8, 20 and 60 Eden years). One body per person for their whole life; the host scales the model by `heightCm` from `body.ts` (broader or slighter by build, shrinking in old age), easing a little each look, instead of swapping child, youth and adult prefabs. The test checks no step of 0.05 Eden year grows by more than 0.04 of the model's height.
- **I3 limp and scars** (`F152_dirt_scar_closeup.png`, the person at the left has a scar on the cheek and one on the arm; a person born with a lame leg now walks with the limp posture and at 0.8 pace, which only a wound did before). Scars are small dark marks on the bones; seen on the cheek, not checked on every place.
- **I5 the Walker** (`F152_walker_you_tab.png`): new **You** tab to choose height, build, skin, hair and tunic (a bad value is refused with its reason, shown in red); the avatar is drawn at that height and breadth, with the tunic and hair colour put on the hunter's kit (Lua `world.wear`) and skin tone as a tint; needs bars (hunger, thirst, tiredness, dirt, health) as the UI kit's Resource bar widgets at the top right and a Key hints widget at the bottom right; the pace from `walkerSpeed` is set on the hunter's CharacterController walk and sprint speeds. J, K, F and the mouse button strike whoever is beside you and T tends them (the creator's own `heal`), each calling `walkerDeed`; H giving calls it as "gave". Tested: strike lowers trust and hurts, tend raises it, the deeds are kept. **Pace was not measured on the avatar**: software GL runs about 0.3 frames a second so walking speed cannot be timed here; the call into the controller is made and checked only as far as the entity having one.
- **I1 settlements, parties and raiders** (`F152_settlement_party_a.png`): each settlement stands on the edge of the land in its own direction (huts by size, a fire, a banner that is red when hostile, a ruin when abandoned, a pin with its name and distance through the UI kit's Waypoints widget). A trading party, raiders or migrants on their last 150 sim seconds walk in as figures (raiders in red) toward the camp, with a notice when they appear. They are placed on the edge of the land, not at the settlement's true distance (2.4 to 4.2 km).
- **J5 bridge and boat** (`F152_bridge_crossing.png`, `F152_water_row_swim_bridge.png`): a bridge is a plank laid from shore to shore, as long as the planks laid, a person on it is held at the deck. The rower and swimmer are set by hand as said above, so a person was not seen *deciding* to cross in the running game; the mind-side test of F147 still covers the decision.
- **J6 the 18 crafted kinds, plus bridge and granary** (`F152_crafted_objects.png`): prefabs for pot, spit, oven, kiln, loom, quern, ball, swing, flute, drum, field, channel, pen, dog bed, well, hive, boat, raft, bridge and granary; a unit test checks every kind a craft makes has a prefab in the scene. **Reused models** from the kit: bucket (pot), rock-medium (oven), anvil-forge (kiln), rock-stone-01 (quern), barrel-large (drum), fence-picket (pen), bedroll (dog bed), planter (well), rowboat-small (boat), farmhouse (granary). **Stand-ins not made for the job**: weapon-rack for the spit, market-stall for the loom, paifang-gate for the swing. **Plain boxes**: ball, flute, field, channel, hive, raft, bridge. In the shot, several are small and not easy to tell apart; the granary was moved out of frame because it fills it.
- **G3 festival** (`F152_festival_evening.png`, `F152_night_fire_lighting.png`): on the evening of a feast day a big fire, four banners, a drum and a flute are set at the camp's place, with people gathered there (the mind's own bias does the gathering; in the shot two people stand at it), and taken away at 22:00. The calendar is the UI kit's Objective widget (the next feast and in how many days, or "Feast day: the Waking, tonight the people gather at the fire") and a Notification widget when the feast begins; no loose text lines. Fires flicker. Only two people gathered in the shots; a crowd was not shown. The dusk shot is dusk, not full night.
- **The layers in the Minds tab** (`F152_minds_layers.png`): sections for the other settlements (and parties on the road), trade and money (prices, money, ledger, market, caravan, debts, granary, ventures), law, leaders and feuds (law strength, ethos, leaders, laws, taboos, cases, feuds, alliances, schisms, wars) and the feasts. The feud in the shot was added by hand to the world.

## Not done, said plainly

- Not seen: the Walker's pace measured in metres a second, a person choosing to row or to cross a bridge in a running game (staged by hand), a crowd at a festival, full night, a person with the limp *walking* (the shot is a still), the blink caught by chance (the shot holds the eyes shut), a scar anywhere but the cheek and arm, the "editable look" of ordinary people (only the Walker's is editable), the ground white with snow.
- Brows move up and down only. No mouth or lids exist on the models.
- Settlement figures walk straight in from the edge; they do not leave again, and what they do on arrival (trade, raid) is shown only by the mind's own notes.
- The crafted prefabs have no animation or use sound; a pot is not seen being cooked in.
- `npm test`: 601 passed before the new unit tests, which pass (16). The full browser suite was not run (no emsdk); `eden.cjs` and `eden_present.cjs` pass, the latter after the last build.
