# F139 Eden phase B (part 2): adolescence, pairing, family, the body's rhythms, middle and old age

New module `eden/lifecycle.ts` (`person.life`), wired into the step loop (`lifeStep`), the options (`arcOptions`), movement (`ageSpeed`), perception (`sightOf`), conception (`canConceive`), birth, death (`afterDeath`, `heirsOf`), and quarrels (`obedience`). Age constants there are plain numbers (see "Fixed on the way").

**Adolescence (B4).** At human 12 (Eden 2.667) puberty arrives: a girl's cycle begins, a boy's voice breaks, each noted and kept in the life story. Obedience to parents falls to about 0.4 at the height of adolescence and recovers (`obedience`); quarrels with a parent are up to 2.5 times as likely; regard for the parents cools and mends; values drift toward friends and away from the parents; autonomy is wanted more. From human 14 a first love forms (near their age, not kin, mostly the other sex, about a tenth the same): infatuation is feeling and reward; a refusal after three tries is a heartbreak (despair, shame, loneliness, serotonin down, stress up, remembered). They take more risks with friends near, doubt what their parents taught (a held belief is dropped), and at the end of adolescence settle who they are: *achieved* if they have tried four kinds of thing and have a role, *moratorium* if two, else *diffused*.

**Young adulthood (B5).** Courtship: someone fond of them, not close kin, of similar values and near their age is chosen; time together moves it on; a mutual regard makes a *pair* (`partner`, recorded, the chronicle, a milestone), the start of a clan (a clan follows the father). Pairs provide for each other, and for a woman carrying or nursing (the man takes up the work she cannot); a pair with wood and no roof of their own sets out to build one. Ambition: esteem wears faster. Role: what they are known for is kept up.

**Middle age (B6).** They teach more; look after an old parent, a grandparent or others where it is a custom (the norm *we look after our old* forms when seen); at the middle of life, once, a crisis of meaning (meaning drive to the floor, despair and regret, a milestone, a few days drawn to ponder, create, pray and teach); deaths among those their age are felt.

**Old age (B7).** After human 60: slower (down to half), sight shortened, frailty; the heavy work (hunt, build, gather, plant) is let go in proportion to frailty; the stories, teaching, prayer and talk are kept and a wish to leave something (legacy); from 62 some lose their memory (places are forgotten, words fail, they ask who you are); when frail they face death serene (meaning, faith, steady mood) or afraid; standing falls with strength; the old are listened to a little more when they speak of what they believe; an old person who is fed and sat with is eased and one who is not grows lonelier.

**The body (B9).** A woman has a cycle (menstrual, follicular, fertile, luteal): she can conceive only in the fertile phase (the chance there is raised to keep the average), is short-tempered before bleeding, tired during; nursing holds the cycle back for a while; menopause ends it; a man and woman have different sex hormones, which tilt the hunt toward men and tending toward women. After a birth there are months in which stress builds faster, more where she is alone or exhausted, and she may fall into a dark spell (a mother in one gives less milk). Childbirth risk for mother and baby is higher when she is hungry or unwell (tested over 500 births each).

**Family (B10).** `closeKin` finds parent, child, sibling, half-sibling, grandparent, grandchild, aunt or uncle, niece or nephew, cousin; `forbidden` bars all but cousins and those raised together (the Westermarck effect); conception needs a recognised partner who is not forbidden. The partner is widowed at a death (grief, mourning for half a year in which they do not court). An orphan (no mother alive) is taken in by relatives first, then by someone fond of them with food to spare (a lactating woman for an infant), and that custom grows when seen; an infant with a lactating guardian is nursed. Step-parents are the partners of parents. Inheritance goes by the people's values: equal shares if fairness weighs most among them, the eldest child if tradition does, the partner if kin does. People answer "who is your husband / what is your family?".

**Fixed on the way.** `lifecycle.ts` read `ADULT_AGE` at load time and crashed any test that imported `mind` first. Ages there are now plain numbers (checked by test), and `tests/edenModules.test.ts` imports each eden module first, alone. Added options yield to the person's own thirst and hunger (parents feed a child from what they carry, they do not go without water to do it); eating, drinking, foraging and fishing gain score as hunger and thirst pass the middle, which brought time in urgent need back from 5.8% to 4.7%. A feature that made the lives test fail at 6.2% was caught by that existing test and fixed; none was edited.

**Measured.** 17 tests in `tests/edenLifecycle.test.ts` and 22 in `tests/edenModules.test.ts`; a long run of twelve young adults for eight years pairs off (at least two pairs), has children (at least two births) and no pregnancy between forbidden kin. Season sweep (4,000 lives): urgent need time 4.7% (limit 6%), died 0.2%; two-year sweep (300 lives): urgent need 4.8%. The same 110 two-year lives run on the code before phase A and now: 7 lives with a death before (all babies of 0.5 to 0.9, illness, wounds and cold), 6 now (babies, one toddler, two adults; wounds and cold): no worse. (The sweep's headline "anyone died" moved from 0.5% to 2.1% of 191 Eden lives, which is 1 to 4 lives, and that comparison shows it is noise.)

**Open in phase B:** the child's body does not grow smoothly (the host swaps models by age); wealth and debt in middle age wait for the economy phase (E); no one yet lives at a different distance from their parents' roof than a build option gives; retirement is only the heavy work being let go.
