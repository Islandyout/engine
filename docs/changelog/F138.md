# F138 Eden phase B (part 1): infancy, early and middle childhood, sensitive periods

New module `eden/development.ts` (`person.dev`), wired into the step loop (`devStep`), the options (`devOptions`), the share case (`witnessShare`), deaths (`onDeathSeenBy`), and the end of each step (`limitSpeech`). An infant is now a baby only until `AGE.infancyEnds` (Eden age 0.444, human 2); after that the old "nursed until 1" rule gives way to weaning, solids, and a child's own choices.

**Infancy (B1).** How well a baby is looked after (a running `care`, from a carer being near when it needs) decides, at the end of infancy, its attachment: *secure*, *anxious* or *avoidant*, with `bondDeficit` if care was very low (oxytocin response capped for life). A baby that needs and has no one near cries (the parents who love it hear, are stressed and go to it: `comfort` option 1.7); being held stops it (touch). It learns object permanence (separation distress after), takes first steps (parents note and feel it), says a first word, is afraid of strangers (more if anxious), and is weaned (milk counts for less over 0.3 to 0.444, mother's food takes over). Attachment style then colours the adult: loneliness 1.6x for the anxious, 0.6x for the avoidant; belonging target; kindness counted for less by the avoidant (`appraise`); jealousy 1.5x for the anxious.

**Early childhood (B2).** Vocabulary grows from the words heard (those spoken to them count three times); what a child can say is limited to its words (babble below 3, two words below 25, five below 80). Grabs food from a grown person when hungry and is corrected (shame, learns *ask first*, and stops grabbing); tantrums when frustrated (hunger, tiredness, loneliness) before about 1.2; afraid of the dark when alone at night; a stage of "mine" (slow to share); some children (about 30%) have an imaginary friend that fades; plays at what adults do and practises the skill a little.

**Middle childhood (B3).** From six they may take chores (`gather`, `forage`, `plant`, `stash`, `fish`); each picks a master among the grown people (by skill, fondness, being kin), follows them and learns their skill faster; peers within a year or so of age play-fight (a standing forms, laughter, an occasional scrape); a child protests "That's not fair!" when food is given to someone else and not to them and the giver shares more evenly; jealous of a sibling who has the mother's attention; takes up the beliefs of the parents; and a death is understood by age: a small child keeps looking, an older one understands it is permanent.

**Sensitive periods (B8).** If a child has heard under 60 words by the end of the speech window (Eden 1.556, human 7), `speechDeficit` is set: later learning is a fifth as effective. If care was under a quarter, the bond deficit sets (above). Both persist.

**Parents (B5, part).** Parents feed a hungry child with what they have first and go for food when they have none.

**Measured.** 14 tests in `tests/edenDevelopment.test.ts` (each failing without the module): held versus unheld baby; the window for bonding; attachment's effects on the adult; first steps, word, permanence, strangers; weaning; vocabulary of a spoken-to and a silent child and the closing window; the speech limit; grab, correction and learning; tantrum; the dark; "mine"; imaginary friends; pretend play; chores by age; master, play-fight, sibling rivalry, beliefs; fairness protest; understanding of death by age; parental feeding; a family raising a child through infancy to the middle of childhood in a long run. Season sweep (4,000 lives): urgent need time 5.4% (limit 6%); two-year sweep (300 lives): 47% have births, 0.5% anyone died, urgent need time 4.3%, all within limits. The earlier full unit suite (340) passes.

**Open in phase B:** adolescence, young adulthood, middle age, old age, women's and men's lives, and family structure (next parts); the child's body does not yet change with the stage beyond what the host already does.
