# F137 Eden phase A: drives, body chemistry, lasting states, wider emotions, habits, memory, life story

New module `eden/psyche.ts` under the choices, wired into the step loop (`psycheStep`), the scoring (`psycheBias`) and the end of every action (`afterAct`).

**Drives (A1).** Eleven: safety, belonging, esteem, autonomy, competence, curiosity, meaning, attachment, intimacy, play, stimulation (boredom is the last, no longer just the `joy` need). Each is a satisfaction that wears down with time (or follows circumstances: a roof or fire for safety, fond people near for belonging, kin near for attachment) and is fed by what they do. What is low is wanted and tilts the scores of what would feed it; the tilt yields to hunger and thirst as they press, so wants never starve anyone.

**Chemistry (A2, A3, A4).** Dopamine, serotonin, oxytocin, cortisol, endorphin, noradrenaline, testosterone, oestrogen (rising at puberty, falling in age, by sex), melatonin (by the dark). Each has a baseline, is raised by events (`psycheEvent`: reward, social, touch, threat, pain, loss, achievement, defeat, novelty, laughter, beauty...) and by feelings, by the body (hunger, cold, tiredness, wounds) and by company, and falls back at its own speed. Mood reads it. Rewards land less each time (tolerance, recovering over days); with tolerance built and the rewards gone there is a craving (`deficit`) that sinks mood and draws them to reward. Stress above a level builds `strain`; past a point it becomes an *anxious* or a *depressed* state that outlasts its cause, makes the anxious jumpy and avoiding and the depressed withdrawn, lowers action, and eases with time, faster in company.

**Emotions (A5).** Eighteen added to the six: love, loneliness, jealousy, envy, guilt, disgust, contempt, hope, despair, awe, surprise, nostalgia, longing, boredom, regret, relief, humour, empathy, each with a named trigger (a partner near; being alone with the need for company high; the loved one giving attention to someone else; a better maker's admired work; a theft or quarrel; an ill person close; low regard; a plan or spring; a depressed state; beauty; a sudden fright; an absent or dead loved one; low stimulation; a plan lost; a fright passing; a joke; a liked person suffering). Pairs give a named blend (`blendOf`): rage, devotion, remorse, humiliation, triumph, desolation and others. Chemistry follows the feelings.

**Habit and the unconscious (A6).** What is done ten times at the same time of day runs by itself (a bonus in scoring); a fright above 0.7 fixes itself to the kind of thing near it (a *cue*): later it fires fear at the sight, fading if nothing happens and deepening if the thing is dangerous, and they avoid it; strong joy fixes a liking to what was near; how an act changes opinion of someone depends on how they already see them (`appraise`: a friend's slip is forgiven, a foe's kindness is doubted, a low state sees kindness less).

**Memory (A7).** Strength of a remembered line falls with age at a rate set by how much it mattered (`memoryStrength`); a cue (a name or word) brings back the matching lines strongest first, shaded by mood (a low mood finds bad things easier; remembering strengthens); dull old lines are forgotten. Recall is used in two places in the game: after two people talk, each recalls what they hold of the other (fondly or darkly by mood, nudging fondness and writing a "brought back" line), and the Walker can ask "do you remember X?" and gets what they actually kept, or that it has faded. Sleep (`consolidate`) sets the day's strongest and lets the weak go, and brings dreams, or a nightmare after a lasting fright or heavy strain.

**Life story (A8).** `person.story` is kept from where they began, the stage they are in (`humanYears`: an Eden child grows up at 4, old at 55), milestones (love, becoming a parent, a loss, a discovery, a lasting low state) and how life is now.

**To the voice and the inspector.** The free voice is told what is under the surface (a lasting low spell, what they badly want, a craving, a named blend); the Minds tab shows body chemistry, any lasting state, what is wanted and what is felt.

**Fixed on the way.** Emotions below 0.01 were deleted at once, so small steady inputs (the ones love and loneliness need) vanished; the threshold is now 0.0002. Two older tests broke: a goal line ("I want to build a shelter") was forgotten after two days by the new forgetting curve (goals now weigh 0.5, so it is kept), and the diet test compared illness counts of 30 people at about 0.03 versus 0.02 a day, which is noise that failed with the order of the random stream; it now checks `deficient()` and `dietFactor()` (the 1.35 hazard factor, extracted so a test can read it). An illness-count comparison was dropped: it needs thousands of lives and the season sweep already measures illness over 4,000.

**Measured (A9).** Unit tests: 20 in `tests/edenPsyche.test.ts`, covering every item above, each failing without the module. Season sweep (4,000 lives): before 4.7% of time in urgent need, after 5.3%, within what a person would do (limit 6%); an early version at 7.1% was caught by the sweep and fixed by making wants yield to thirst and hunger. 60 people for 3 years (`tools/eden/bigworld.ts`, seed 1): baseline end 60 of 60, 3.05% of person-time desperate (seed 2: 2.87%); with psyche end 60 of 60, 3.15%. The baseline is the code at commit 1c627a1 (before psyche), run with the same `bigworld.ts`. Cost: 650 s against 407 s for the whole run. A CPU profile of the 60-person run puts the psyche functions (`psycheStep`, `psycheBias`, the `bondOf` calls they make) at about a fifth of the time; the rest of the rise is the larger emotion set looped through every step by `fade` and `moodPush`. `psycheStep` now rules out partners cheaply (a partner needs affinity above 0.8) and checks for first love once a minute.

**Still open in this phase:** envy and contempt have thin triggers (envy: a better maker's admired work while esteem is low; contempt: low regard nearby); "biases" is the one appraisal function (used when people talk), not yet the whole of perception; the psyche does not yet depend on childhood (phase B).
