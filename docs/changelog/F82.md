# F82 — GATEBREAKER M2: the loop (0.82.0)

GATEBREAKER's second milestone ([GAME_DESIGN.md §4 and §10](../gatebreaker/GAME_DESIGN.md)): a hub, saves, quests, three Gates and the first rank-up. The scene is `examples/gatebreaker/gatebreaker.json` (built by `tools/gatebreaker/build_game.ts`; it replaces `e-rank-gate.json`). A full session, from the prologue to rank D, takes about an hour.

## The game

- **The hub**: after the first Gate the plaza becomes the Hunter Association's square. Walk up to a station and press **G**:
  - the **Gate Board** lists the Gates you can enter, and why the others are locked;
  - **Smith Kang** turns gold and goblin fangs into dagger upgrades (+1 to +5, +8% damage each);
  - the **training mat** holds the **Daily Quest**: land 20 hits, dodge 5 swings and land a Heavy within 2:30 for a stat point and 50 G;
  - the **door home** ends the day and saves. Skip the Daily Quest and the Ledger sends you into a 60-second **penalty quest** first.
- **Three Gates**, each with three rooms and a boss, played again as often as you like:
  - **Goblin Cave** (E): the first Gate, with the tutorial on the first run.
  - **Subway Tunnel** (E): hobgoblins that break your guard, shamans that heal their allies, and the **Hobgoblin Brute**. Clearing it the first time teaches **Fang Whirl** (E).
  - **Goblin Fortress** (D): the rank test. The **Goblin Warlord** calls its guard in phase 2. It opens at Lv.5.
- **Rank-up**: clearing the Fortress plays a short panel ceremony, and Han Seo-jin is D-rank.
- **Stats that matter**: STR raises damage, AGI attack and dodge speed, VIT max health, INT mana and skill damage, SEN the critical hit chance. **C** opens the status window, which shows each stat's effect in numbers and spends points. Level-ups give 3 points.
- **XP, gold and fangs** from every kill, and a reward window when a Gate is cleared.
- **The Ledger pulls you out** of a Gate when your health falls too low (after the first Gate). You keep the XP from your kills, but get no clear reward.
- **Story quests** on the hub's objective line: clear the Cave, upgrade your daggers, clear the Tunnel, reach Lv.5, pass the D-rank test.
- **Saves**: level, XP, stats, gold, fangs, upgrades, rank, skills, quests and the day survive a reload. A saved game starts in the hub.

## Weapons held properly

Fighters now hold weapons the way game engines equip them: a socket on the skeleton, a grip on the weapon, and a grip type for each weapon (`apps/editor/src/editor/heldWeapons.ts`).

- **Grip types**: swords, axes and knives in a forward grip (blade out of the thumb side, edge toward the knuckles). The hunter's daggers in a reverse grip, along the forearm. Shields strapped to the forearm, face out. Claymores, spears and scythes held in both hands, with the bow in the off hand.
- **Real sizes and grips**: each weapon has a length in metres and the points on it where the hands go, measured from the model's handle and guard. Before, every weapon was squeezed to one of three lengths and gripped 13% up its bounding box.
- **Two-handed weapons**: two-bone IK puts the left hand on the handle every frame, after the animation. A two-hander wielded with a shield (the Goblin Warlord) is held one-handed.
- **Stances by weapon**: a fighter with a one-handed blade or a shield stands in the sword guard and blocks with the blade. Fists, daggers, two-handers and bows keep the boxing guard, the one stance where both hands are close together.

## For people working on the engine

- `melee.tune(damage, speed, crit, skill, health_max, mana_max)` sets a fighter's stats. Critical hits deal 1.5x and set a `crit` flag on the hit event; the editor letters them **CRIT!**.
- **Scripts talking to each other**: every script compiles before any `on_start` runs, so a script can `world.send` to one further down the entity list from its first tick; before, that message was lost. `world.send` also keeps an integer an integer (it arrived as `3.0`, so `"G" .. n` read `G3.0`).
- **Light pool**: past 8 point lights, the nearest 8 to the camera stand in for all of them, so the shader's light count stays fixed. The three Gates hold dozens of torches.
- The boss bar also finds spawned bosses (prefab instances).
- `SceneBuilder.prefab()` in `tools/gatebreaker/kit.ts`: enemies are prefabs that the Director spawns room by room.
- New browser test `tests/browser/gatebreaker_loop.cjs`: a saved Lv.5 hunter runs the hub stations, the penalty quest, the drill, the status window, the D-rank test and the rank-up, then reloads to check the save. Both GATEBREAKER browser tests share `gatebreaker_bot.cjs`.
