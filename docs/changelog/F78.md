# F78 — Martial arts (0.78.0)

Adds hand-to-hand combat: a **Melee** component with combos, kicks, specials, dodges, blocks and parries, driven by motion-captured and hand-keyed martial-arts clips. Hits come with hit-stop, impact effects and slow motion, and the AI fighters take turns. The guide is [COMBAT.md](../COMBAT.md).

## For people making games

- **Melee component** (Gameplay group). Choose a style:
  - **Martial arts**: four-punch and three-kick chains, a power hook, a launcher, a knee, air punch and kick, a dash attack, roll and slide dodges, and four specials.
  - **Sword**: light and heavy chains, a kick, a dash cut, a rising cut, a piercing dash and a slam.
  - **Custom**: write your own move list as `name: key=value ...`.

  The fighter can be the Player or an AI fighter, and you set its team, aggression, skill, reaction, starting energy and guard.
- **Fighting**:
  - Combos come from an input buffer and per-move cancel windows.
  - Specials take direction sequences (`f`, `b`, `bf`) and energy built by landing hits.
  - There are air and running variants.
  - Dodges have invulnerable frames, and a dodge can cancel a move after its blow.
  - A held block soaks blows from the front, draining a guard that breaks.
  - A parry window, launches, juggles with damage scaling, knockdowns, invulnerable get-ups and armored moves.
- **Feel**:
  - Each move's clip is scrubbed in step with the simulation.
  - A boxing guard is held over strafing legs.
  - Reactions are matched to the hit: head or body snaps, knockback, launch, air hits, the fall, kip-up or get-up, guard break, and death.
  - Hit-stop with a shudder, sparks, flashes and limb trails, rings and shockwaves, camera shake, and slow motion for parries, perfect dodges, finishers and knockouts.
  - Synthesized whooshes, thuds, blocks, the parry ring and body falls.
  - Lock-on (T) with a camera that frames both fighters, and a fighter HUD: health, energy, guard, a combo counter and a lock marker.
- **AI fighters**: close in, circle at striking range, wait their turn when another fighter is already on you, string combos that end in kicks or heavies, use specials, and block, parry or dodge by skill.
- **New input actions** in the defaults: `light`, `heavy`, `kick`, `special`, `dodge`, `block`, `lock` (see [COMBAT.md](../COMBAT.md)). The old F-key overlap punch is skipped for a fighter, which kicks with F instead.
- **Martial-arts dojo** starter under **New**: you against a sparring partner and a sensei, with a training dummy.
- **Scripts**:
  - a `melee` table: `perform`, `state`, `move`, `set_energy`, `lock`, `target`, `set_ai`;
  - an `on_melee_hit(target, move, damage, outcome)` callback.
- **Template fixes** (all starters):
  - The ground was a body below y = 0, so on the first tick it was lifted onto the ground plane and flung whoever stood on it. It is kinematic now.
  - CameraFollow only steers an entity with a Camera, so each starter now has its own follow camera instead of a CameraFollow on the Player that did nothing.
  - The first-person targets' Health used `max` instead of `maximum`.
  - A test now catches all three.
- Scene-wide entities (UI labels, the Environment, post-processing, audio and input settings) no longer show a stand-in box during Play.

## Animation sources

- `assets/source/kit/people/combat_clips.glb` (55 clips, 2.9 MB) is built by `tools/models/import_combat_clips.mjs`. It holds a bare skeleton and animations, and is loaded the first time a scene has a fighter.
  - **Quaternius Universal Animation Library 1 and 2** (CC0): strikes, reactions, rolls, slides, jumps, strafing, sword sets and specials. They are on the same skeleton as Mannequin F and the Talari, so they are copied directly. Each strike is joined to its recovery; scale tracks and bone translations below the pelvis are dropped.
  - **CMU Graphics Lab Motion Capture Database** (free for any use): front, roundhouse and side kicks (both legs) and a knife-hand block from subject 135's karate recordings. They are cut out by foot height, turned so the strike points ahead, and retargeted. The retarget transfers world-space rotations bone by bone, with a rest-pose alignment for CMU's splayed legs.
- The tool also measures each clip's root-motion travel and strike frame. The default move lists take their lunges and hit windows from those numbers.

## For people working on the engine

- `engine::gameplay` melee (`include/engine/gameplay/melee.hpp`, `melee.cpp`) is pure and unit tested (`engine_melee_tests`). It holds:
  - the move-list parser;
  - the fighter state machine (`update_fighter`, `force_move`);
  - hit resolution (`resolve_hit`, `take_hit`, `land_hit`);
  - lunge speed;
  - the melee brain (`think`).
- The runtime's `Fighter` component and `editor.melee` system are in `apps/editor/runtime/bridge_melee.cpp`:
  - Player input or brain, facing and lock-on, lunges and knockback slides, hit spheres, projectiles, landing.
  - A defeated fighter lies there before it's removed.
  - Exports: `editor_set_melee`, `editor_fighter_value`, `editor_fighter_text`, `editor_take_melee_events` and `editor_melee_event`, with named `FighterField` and `MeleeEventField` fields.
  - The Lua host's `melee` hook is here too.
- The editor's side is `combatView.ts`. The default move lists are mirrored in `meleeMoves.ts`, and a test keeps them equal to the engine's.
- Tests:
  - the bridge suite fights: chains, kicks and knockback, parry and block, launch-land-get-up, an AI fighter, defeat;
  - the editor suite checks the stance and reaction picks, the move-list mirror and that every named clip exists;
  - the template suite now checks component fields, cameras and grounds.

## Limits

- The feet aren't IK-pinned. Lunges follow the clips' measured root motion, so sliding is small but not zero.
- The sword style has no sword model yet.
- Hits are spheres against boxes.
