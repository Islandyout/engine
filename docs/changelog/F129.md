# F129 Eden: motion with a reason, and a check over 30,000 situations

**What every body shows now comes from one place, `eden/motion.ts`,** which turns what a mind is doing and feeling into a clip and a pose, with no randomness of its own. The page uses it, and so does the check below.

**The check** (`tools/eden/animflow.ts`, `npx tsx ../../tools/eden/animflow.ts 30000`; 4 minutes): runs thousands of situations (2 to 6 people of all ages, hungry, cold, hurt, grieving, angry; deer, foxes, wolves, horses, cows, dogs, stags, alpacas, donkeys; any hour, season and weather) through the same mind-to-motion code and counts the gaps: arriving somewhere and doing nothing that shows it, feelings no one could see, poses that flicker, jumps from sleep to work, animals that glide, slide or bounce between clips, the one spoken to not listening, laughing in grief, talking to someone asleep, a body stuck in one pose for hours. The first run found, among others, 5,440 arrivals with nothing shown, 14,000 talks nobody was listening to, 67,953 animal clip flickers and 26,524 paces at the limit.

**Fixed because of it**
- People: a pause or a wander's end is looking about; a person walked up to stops and listens (and goes on after); they stretch on waking, before anything else; sad, angry, afraid, ill, cold, tired, hungry, proud people each show it (slump, tense jaw and fists, alert crouch, hugged arms with a shiver, a yawn, a hand on the belly, chest out), steady and without flicker; no one wakes a sleeper to chat; a starving person with food eats first.
- Humour: friendly people tell a joke (about what they have seen, the rain, hunger, themselves) and the two laugh together, which lifts both and is remembered; someone grieving, angry or afraid does not laugh. Serious talk (grief, fear, anger) keeps its serious body even while speaking.
- Animals: each creature has a brain state (grazing, watching, drinking, resting, walking, fleeing, hunting, following, pouncing, eating a kill) and a clip chosen for its species and the clips it really has (deer, horses, cows and donkeys graze and lower their heads; stags, alpacas and wolves have a second idle and an eating clip; a wolf pounces then eats; foxes and dogs use what they have), played at a pace matched to its speed so the feet do not glide. The pause starts when it gets there, is long at night, and a startled animal keeps running a few seconds after the danger is out of sight.
- The `Intent` of someone listening carries what they were doing (`resume`) to take up again.

After the fixes, the same 30,000 situations (30 million looks at people, 37 million at animals) leave 468 people talked to while asleep, 100 pose flickers and 17 laughs in a bad mood, and nothing else; the season sweep (3,000 lives) shows no new deaths or stuck lives.

Seen in screenshots on the new bodies: sit, sleep, eat, harvest, chop, slump, hugged arms against the cold, tense fists, an alert crouch, a hand to the mouth, a hand on the belly, the wave. Alpacas now get bodies. In the live world creatures play their clips at their pace (a deer walks at 0.8, a startled one runs at 0.7, a stag eats, a wolf rests head low).

Not yet: a lying-down clip (the pose layer does it), stag and alpaca headbutts and kicks, herds that move together, animals' own ageing animations.
