# F135 Eden: thinking ahead and finding things out (space, time, plans, experiments)

The people reacted to the moment, scoring what to do next from hunger, thirst and mood. They now also reason about **where** things are and **when** things happen, make plans and carry them out, and invent. New module `eden/reason.ts`; new intents `make` and `invent`.

**Space and time.** A person works from what they know: the places in their memory and the distances between them, how fast they walk, the hour, seconds until dark (`secsToDark`, from the day's real sunset) and until winter (`secsToWinter`), and what they carry.

**Plans.** `foresee` turns that into a plan with steps, a time estimate and a deadline, written in their own words in their journal and shown in the Minds tab:
- *A fire for the night:* dusk is under about three world hours off, it is cold, there is no fire at camp: fetch wood from the nearest known wood (distance and walking time reckoned), return, light the fire before dark.
- *A full larder for winter:* in summer or autumn, count the larder against what the household needs and the days left, and plan the food trips and the stashing.
- *Make what they know:* with a thin larder, use an invention they know (weir, snare, net).
Steps are carried out as ordinary intents, scored above leisure and below real need (hunger, thirst or tiredness over about 0.35 pull the score down, over 0.65 switch it off), noticed as done by the state of things, and dropped, with a note, when late or taking too long. Asked "what are you planning?", a person says what they have worked out.

**Experiments.** When food is short and nothing known helps, or just from curiosity on a free day, a person tries a combination (how much wood, with or without a hide, by water or by a game trail), spending the wood. Wrong guesses are remembered and not repeated. Three laws of this world nobody is told (culture.ts `INVENTIONS`): a **weir** (4 wood by water; fills with fish while there are fish), a **snare** (3 wood by a game trail; fills with meat while game is near), a **net** (2 wood and a hide; fishing catches half again as much and 3 food a catch). A discovery is a chronicle entry, spreads by conversation, goes into books, and is lost if nobody holds it and no book keeps it.

**Measured.** Ten-year worlds of 2–4 people: 30–170 plans each with 88–97% completed; a fire lit before dark in the unit test; inventions found in four of five worlds. Season sweep (4,000 lives) and year sweep (300): within what a person would do (urgent need time 4.7%, was 4.3% before). Fixed on the way: the sweep's winter-hunger measure divided by zero when everyone had died.

**Not done:** planning with more than one other person (no shared plans yet), plans that cross to unexplored ground, an LLM as a planner (the structure is there: a plan is plain data, so a model could propose steps for the planner to check).
