# F105 Eden: a wider valley and an inner life

Phases 1 and 2 of the "People of Eden" design.

## The valley

- The scene is 400 m across (`tools/eden/build_eden.ts`): the river runs 330 m from the highlands to a lake, with an east oak wood, a pinewood, an old orchard, a southern meadow, wolf-haunted highlands and dirt tracks from the garden. Woods use the low-poly kit trees so a forest stays light to draw.
- New places (`places.ts`) are food, rest, wood and view sources for the minds (woodland, orchard, pinewood, peak, lake), with berry bushes spread through the woods and meadows. A place can have a `stand` spot (the lake bank).
- Herds of deer in three regions, horses and alpacas on the grass, wolves in the hills. Herd caps grow with the world's area.
- Curious people set out on expeditions to ground they have not seen.
- The Creator's place names match within a word, not across words ("moon" no longer finds "south meadow").

## Inner life (`apps/editor/src/eden/inner.ts`)

- Named emotions (joy, fear, anger, gratitude, pride, shame) rise from what happens, fade at their own speeds and pull the mood. Bold people are frightened less, patient people slower to anger.
- Mood falls faster than it rises. Held low long enough it breaks: a bold impatient person loses their temper with whoever is near, others need to be alone.
- Falling out is no longer rare: friction comes from temperament, anger, low moods and the last meal being eaten in front of a hungry person. A quarrel can be made right by an apology.
- What people do for one another becomes what they think of them (generous, kind, quick to anger), and is passed on as gossip, shifting trust.
- Journal lines carry an importance; forgetting removes the least important old ones, and people tell the thing that mattered most.
- The Minds tab shows what they feel and what they have come to believe.

Measured on 150 two-year lives: groups that fell out 91%, made up 86%, formed opinions 68%, gossiped 58%, broke down 2%; nobody died in Eden-like climates.
