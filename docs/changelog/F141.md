# F141: Eden phase D, love, pain, joy and meaning

`heart.ts`. **Love:** a person who likes someone a great deal falls for them (infatuation: dopamine and longing, about three days), which settles into attachment if returned and becomes longing, then fades after about twenty days, if not (and they do not fall for the same person again). A bonded person may stray (more likely with a cold bond and loose values); an affair is a fact, and the partner is hurt only when they come to know it. The first betrayal wounds trust and may be forgiven; the second ends the bond and love turns bitter over days. Someone who loves a child or partner steps between them and a beast, and is hurt. **Pain:** body pain (wound, illness, extreme hunger/cold) becomes a chronic ache if it goes on and leaves a fear that lowers hunting and stealing and raises rest; humiliation, being alone for days and watching a loved one hurt without being able to help also wear on mood and strain. **Joy:** recorded by source (meaning, achievement after hardship, belonging, laughter, generosity, beauty, play, ritual, celebration); births and pairings are celebrated by those within 40 m; giving while hungry yourself is tallied as costly kindness. **Meaning:** a purpose (family, craft, knowledge, faith, community) is found from who they are, steadies them against strain, and when lost puts them in crisis; in crisis they pray, ponder and read; the faithful are consoled by ritual and the crisis is shorter.

**Not done, said plainly.** No unrequited-love pining that changes daily behaviour beyond a bias to seek them out; no costly friendship beyond the tally; no abuse after-effects (Phase H); "giving life for something" is only the sacrifice step against animals, and nobody dies of it in a test; music and dance are not modelled as acts; joy sources are tallied but not shown in the UI.
