# F150: Eden society and learning, finished so they emerge in the running sim

Follows F143 (learning) and F144 (society), whose "not done" lists this closes. New: `src/eden/folklore.ts` (data only, no imports); changes in `society.ts`, `learning.ts`, and small hooks in `conflict.ts`, `heart.ts`, `speech.ts`, `mind.ts` (`societyAfter` at the end of an act), `culture.ts` (`Work.tale`). `tools/eden/society_measure.ts` prints the tallies below. Chances come from hashes, new options yield to thirst and hunger.

**Leaders have power.** Each day the leader looks at the people's need (a feast, the store before winter, wood, a roof, fish) and orders trusting followers to do it (`econ.order`, which the economy bias already honours); a roof needs the council to agree; followers who distrust the leader or are set against being told refuse (and cool to them). At each feast the leader collects an offering of one food from members with plenty (refusers are noted), the feast is eaten from it, and the common store is shared out to the hungry who have nothing. A camp keeps this store in its shelter's stock.
**More than theft is judged.** Cases now have kinds: theft, harm (conflict.ts violence, cheating, fraud...: a people with a leader takes it to its own court, which weighs it and carries out conflict.ts's own penalties: warning, restitution, jail, exile) and taboo (working on a feast day, seen by a neighbour, judged by the priest: an offering, then shame, exile on the third breach). After the first judged harm the priest declares a second taboo (no blood among those who share a fire), which makes later harm judged harder.
**Festivals and rites can be observed.** `societyView(world)` returns the calendar, today's and the next feast (`nextFestival`), each group's leader, council, priest, laws, taboos, camp, store and words, the recent feasts and an event list (feasts, rites, funerals, rulings, orders, bans, arbitrations, conversions, splits, collapses; `world.society.events`, newest 300). On a feast day people with food carry it to the site, the evening draws them to the fire (tallied apart: `festivalGatherings` against `eveningGatherings`), the taboo keeps hands from work, tribute is gathered and the feast eaten.
**Feuds** now also start from violence (every act conflict.ts logs), affairs and betrayals (heart.ts), and the priests' own quarrel in a schism; the source is tallied (`feudsFrom_*`).
**Priests** officiate: a rite (naming, coming of age, wedding) waits for the priest, who walks to it, and is held by the elders without them after a day (`ritesOfficiated`, `ritesLay`); they judge breaches of taboo, arbitrate a feud between houses not their own (trusted by both, once a season), and in a schism the two priests each win a doubtful adult from the other side every season (`conversions`), and may ban the rival side's work.
**A camp is a home.** A split builds a shelter with a larder (the founders put in half of what they carry) and a fire, all founders know them; at night their sleep is taken at the camp's roof; carried food is stashed there.
**Speech uses the group's words** (`inTongue`): once a people's words have drifted from the first ones, its lines use its words for water, food, fire, friend, hello and the dead (`speechInDialect`).
**Class arises** (`assignClass`): a child takes the elite if either parent is (rank, or an owner's wealth), with a one-in-ten fall; a founder or a commoner who comes to rank or ownership is raised. Access depends on it: a traditional elite leader and the lettered elite close reading to themselves (`restrict.read`), and a craft whose masters are all of one sex closes to the other (`restrict["skill:x"]`).
**Censorship happens**: a priest bans the work of the other side of a schism, a traditional leader a book of ideas the people did not have (`banWorks`); a banned song or story's tale is no longer told.
**Collapse is an event** (`collapseWatch`): five or more deaths and a quarter of the people in a quarter-year, or a severe epidemic (0.55) over 30% sick, at most once a year, makes people lose knowledge (libraries burn when it is bad).
**Tales**: 26 rules (each tied to an experience: scare, want, cold, illness, grief, betrayal, a quarrel, theft, birth...), 25 false and 10 true beliefs, 16 scenes (`folklore.ts`); a song or story in `world.works` carries a rule (`work.tale`, `tale.work`), made either way round.

**Measures** (`tools/eden/society_measure.ts 40 1 2`: 40 people, one year, seed 2; before = F144/F143 code, after = this change). Before: leaders 6, laws 9, festivals 4, sermons 1, taboos 2, schisms 8, splits 2, rites 40/40/18 (naming/adulthood/wedding), no orders, tribute, court cases, officiated rites, bans, conversions or class. After: leaders 4, laws 5, leaderOrders 14 (47 obeyed, 34 refused), tributeCollected 1, tributeShared 5, feastMeals 3, festivals 7, festivalGatherings 80, casesFiled 209 (harm 132, taboo 60), penalties restitution 103 and jail 3, rulings guilty 106 / dismissed 15, taboos 5, ritesOfficiated 93 of 97, bannedByPriest 4, booksBanned 4, readingClosed 1, conversions 120, schisms 7, splits 2, camp nights at the camp 632 of 1592 person-nights (sleeping at the camp 649), speechInDialect 22, workTales 1, tales 60 (song 33, story 23, myth 3, proverb 1), classRisen 24. Desperate share 2.3% (before 1.9%), population 40 to 43.

Full suite 619 pass; tsc clean. Season sweep (1500 lives): urgent need 5.1% (limit 6%), "All measures within what a person would do".

**Still missing, said plainly.** Festival and rite events are readable (`societyView`) but no panel in the editor draws them. Tribute collected is small (few carry three food). The harm court is busy (132 cases a year): most are cheats and frauds, penalised by restitution; exile and execution did not occur in the 40-person year, only in tests. A camp's people are at the camp only 40% of nights: they still forage far afield and sleep where night finds them if no roof is near. The priest only contends by conversion; no excommunication, holy war or heresy trial. Class is two-valued (elite, common); caste, dowry and marriage barriers are not modelled. Sex closures come only from a craft's masters being one sex. Dialect covers six words and is not used by the Creator's voice. No collapse happened in the 40-person year (no epidemic or mass death occurred); it is exercised by tests.

**After merging with the mind pass.** Friends among the people now often sat as judge for each other and dismissed harm ("a friend must have had a reason"), so a judge who is the accused's friend now stands aside if anyone else can hear the case (`judgeFor`). The G2 test passes unchanged.
