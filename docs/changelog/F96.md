# F96 — Azure Cloud Sect, a murim cultivation game, and `physics.set_gravity_scale` (0.93.0)

- **New game** `games/my-games/azure-cloud-sect` (generated by `cd apps/editor && npm run murim`, from `tools/murim/`): an open-world murim slice.
  - A mountain valley with a sect compound (tiered pagodas, gate, lanterns, training ground), a bandit camp, a shrine and a boss, Elder Mu, and deer.
  - **Cultivation** is Lua on the Player (`tools/murim/lua/player.lua`): hold M still to gather qi, B with a Breakthrough Pill to break through four realms (a hit during the attempt is a qi deviation), H drinks a Qi Elixir. Each realm raises damage, speed, crit, health and mana (`melee.tune`) and unlocks a qi art (`melee.unlock`): Qi Palm, Wind Palm, Tiger Rush, Nine Dragons.
  - **Flying sword** from Foundation: V toggles it; it spends qi, and moves along the camera.
  - Quests are an Objectives list (reach, script, kill goals); the bag is the Inventory widget (I). Enemies are Hunters in tinted gear, the boss has a stagger bar.
  - The HUD is widgets only: one **Fighter bars** widget (HP, mana, ultimate, guard), Boss bar, Objective, Notification, Inventory, Damage numbers, plus the realm and qi readout above the fighter bars. The scene sets `hud: "widgets"`, so no widget is added behind its back.
- **Engine**: `physics.set_gravity_scale(scale[, id])` (1 normal, 0 floats, negative rises); a non-finite scale fails loudly. Native test in `script_tests.cpp`. Docs: SCRIPT_API.md.
- **Found while building it** (not changed here): a Script's `props` only reach Lua when the source declares them with `-- @prop name default`; other characters on the Talari model looked thin at the Player's scale, so the NPCs use the Hunter and Mannequin models.
- **HUD layout** (Minimal style): everything inside a 5% safe margin. Fighter bars bottom-left with the realm and qi line above them, Objective top-left, Minimap top-right, Compass top-centre, Waypoints for the elder, camp and shrine, and the skill slots (1-4) bottom-centre. Controls are not a text box: O or Esc opens the engine's settings panel, which lists the key bindings.
- **Skill slot labels follow the scene's bindings.** The slots were drawn as Q, E, R, F whatever the scene bound; `CombatView.setSlotKeys` now reads `skill1`..`skill3` and `ultimate` from InputActions. Settings labels for the new actions (meditate, breakthrough, elixir, fly, controls).
- **Qi effects**: a glow while meditating or casting, a glowing core and trail for the Qi Palm that flashes and sets the target alight where it lands, and a flash for Wind Palm and Nine Dragons, with sound and camera shake (`tools/murim/lua/qifx.lua`).
- Browser test `tests/browser/murim.cjs`: the game loads, starts as a Mortal, holding M gathers qi, one Fighter bars widget and no Health widget over it.
