# F149: Eden phases E and H, finished in the running sim

An audit found that ventures, hiring, lending, speculation, quotes, price series, ledger balances, the Gini, `betrayAlly` and `splitBand` had no caller in `step()`; only tests called them. This pass gives each a caller, driven by a person's own situation and a hash chance, yielding to thirst and hunger.

**Choices (`economy.ts`, `chooseDay`, once a day per adult, only in a camp of six or more).** Wood to spare starts a venture (a mill, a boat once boats are known) or goes into an open one (more readily where the founder is trusted); the bold buy shares of a built venture from a holder at the going share price (`buyShares`, then `speculate`); someone with food or wood to spare hires a poor neighbour at half a food a day; a worker with a full pack leaves; an employer short of food `command`s a worker to forage; a rich neighbour lends before being asked. Credit now reads the books (`ledgerBalance`: a borrower who already owes over 6 is refused); the fair note quotes the price (`quote`); the patient wait out a low food price (`priceSeries`); the Gini is kept as a series every fourth day. At the fair a bold, curious person brokers between a seller and a buyer.
**Savings are goods.** `saveGoods` replaces the number: surplus food (beyond a pack of 3) and wood (beyond 6) go into `PersonEcon.store`, food spoils there at the shelter rate (the ground rate if they own no shelter), and it comes out when a day made less than a day's keep, when hungry with an empty pack, and to pay a debt falling due. `wealthOf` counts the store. `saveDay` and `capital` remain as the accounting the older tests use.
**Prices in the swap.** At an open fair the food-for-wood swap uses the going prices (`marketRates`), else the old `tradeRates`.
**Land.** An owned shelter, field, garden, crop or snare owns a plot round it (`landOwnerAt`). Building or sowing on another's plot, by someone who is neither kin nor trusted, leaves the thing theirs (`trespass`, trust falls); builders avoid others' plots.
**Bondage runaway (found and fixed).** Cause: every meal from an owned crop or shelter by a non-kin was a theft with its own restitution debt (366 in a year for 40 people), each defaulted and became bonded work (37 bondages, 14 bonded at the end). Now: a friend the owner trusts (0.5) is let eat (`ownerLeave`); no debt is asked of someone hungry enough to be eating (0.5); one open restitution debt per pair, growing, and a new one at most every 16 days; only a loan binds; restitution debts do not make someone "poor".
**A second band without outsiders (`conflict.ts`, `bandSplit`).** At 16 or more adults, with a quarrel, want, a grudge or 20 or more people, a faction (the boldest with the fewest warm ties, and the adults warm to them) goes its own way as a named band; children follow the parent's band; society's camps are bands too. Contact breeds prejudice (0.05 a day, falling with cross-band friends), so raids and war follow with or without scarcity. `betrayAlly` is now called from `conflictStep`: a dishonest, unfair person gives an ally up to someone who holds a grudge against that ally (or a feared band).

**Births.** In a 40-person, one-year run births are limited by the gestation of 0.75 year (only conceptions in the first quarter are born in time): 2 births before phase E, 1 now. Half-year conceptions: 7 before, 5 to 8 now; pairs 13 and 14. I found no layer that suppresses conception. The one real leak was ownership grievances wearing down the trust a pair needs (partner needs trust over 0.65): fixed by the leave above, with a test. The 60-person three-year births comparison was not completed.

**Tests** (`tests/edenEmerge.test.ts`, 13, all through `step()` with day hops): venture started, funded, built and paying; shares traded and speculated; hiring and wages; lending; store in, spoil, out; Gini series above 0.15 in a camp of near-equals; market-priced swap; trespass; bondage and debts under repeated eating from a field (old rules: 58 bondages, 75 debts; now 0 and 10); a camp splits into bands; two bands in want raid and war; betrayal; a partner eating from the other's field stays a partner. They fail on the old code (the new exports and tallies do not exist; the bondage number measured on old code).

**Measured** (`tools/eden/tally.ts 40 1 2`, 40 people, one year, seed 2; before = commit 8c8251f). Before: births 1, loans 1, hired 0, ventures 0, bondage 37, betrayals 0, bands 1, raids 0, wars 0, kills 0, Gini 0.32 (14 bonded). After: births 1, loans 0, hired 2, wages paid 21, ventures started 1 (3 investments, none built), shares traded 0, bandsSplit 1, raids 2, wars 2, betrayals 3, fights 4, kills 0, brokered 38, bondage 0, stored 6, trespass 74, Gini 0.78 (22 poor, 16 owner, 2 workers). Season sweep 1500: 'All measures within what a person would do', urgent need 5.1%. `bigworld.ts 60 1 1`: 60 to 61, no deaths, desperate 2.1%. `npm test`: 613 pass.

**Not emergent, said plainly.** People hold almost no surplus (mean 0.28 food in the pack), so lending to the poor (0 in the year), built ventures, speculation and crashes happen in tests but not in the 40-person year; hiring is rare. The Gini of 0.78 comes mostly from nearly everyone having nothing, not from compounding. A year is 16 days, so debts (8 + 4 days) and wars barely complete. A hired worker's output is credited to the employer without being taken from the worker's own, which makes a little food. No kills in the year (raid wounds rarely reach the death threshold). The split is by size and quarrel, with no walk to a new camp. About 370 meals from others' crops a year are still counted as takings: crops are the camp's food. Strangers are still off unless the page turns them on.
