# F95 — Enterable cars, and bodies the size of their models (0.92.0)

- **Enterable vehicles** ([VEHICLES.md](../VEHICLES.md)): `Vehicle.enterable` (default on) and `Vehicle.enterRadius` (default 2 m). Walking up to an Arcade car shows the Interact prompt (`E: Enter <name>`); E seats the Player (the walker hides inside, the camera and HUD follow the car); E again gets out beside the car with the walker's collider and body type restored. If both sides are blocked the Player stays seated and the prompt says why. The key is the rebindable interact action (a pickup or crate in reach takes it first).
- An enterable car with no `Collider` fails the commit with its name and the reason. The old E-handover existed but had no setting, prompt, tests or docs.
- **Lua**: `vehicle.enter(id)`, `vehicle.exit()`, `vehicle.driver(id)`; scripts hear `on_ui("vehicle", "enter:<name>" | "exit:<name>" | "blocked:<name>")`.
- **Recipe**: Vehicle: enterable car.
- **Collider fix** ([PHYSICS.md](../PHYSICS.md)): a catalog model placed with no `Scale` was drawn at its native size but collided as a 1 m cube, so the player passed through buildings and cars that had a Collider. Its body now takes the model's size.
- Tests: bridge (prompt, enter, drive, exit, walker restored, enterable off, radius, blocked exit, Lua API and events, no-Collider failure), browser `car.cjs` and `collider.cjs`.
