# F130 Eden: errands and requests between you and the people

**You ask them.** In the talk box (E beside someone): "bring me water" / "fetch me some food" / "get me wood" (or the new "Bring me water" button). They go if they are willing (they know you a little or are sociable by nature; not starving, parched, exhausted or too small) and know where to get it (the nearest river or lake, grove or bush, wood they remember; they cannot fetch what they have never seen). They walk there, take it, carry it back with the carrying walk, and put it in your hands: water and food heal you, wood goes in your pack. It is written in their journal and the chronicle and they trust you a little more. If they cannot find you in fifteen minutes, or a real need or danger comes first, they say so and stop.

**They ask you.** A person near you (within 9 m) who is parched, hungry with nothing in hand, or freezing with no wood asks you for it, in their own words, once in a while ("Walker, I am parched. Is there any water you could spare?"). It shows in the Life log, with a hint in the box under the screen.

**You give.** Q gathers what is here: fills your waterskin at the river or lake, picks up wood, picks fruit at the grove (and kills still give meat). H hands over what the nearest person asked for, then what they need most, from what you carry; the hint line shows who is asking and what you carry. Water eases thirst, fruit and meat hunger, wood lets them build a fire. They are grateful, trust you more, others who see it think the better of you, and it goes in the chronicle.

Tested in 3,000 mixed situations with the Walker about (1,938 errands started, 98.6% delivered, none stuck), in unit tests, and in the browser (Eve fetched water from the river and brought it).
