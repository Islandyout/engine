# F100: base characters and a wider clip library

Azure Cloud Sect's people were the engine's bare mannequin in clothes: no face, no hair, flat white skin. They are now Quaternius's CC0 Universal Base
Characters (a textured male and female body with eyes and eyebrows), with hairstyles rigged to the head bone and clothes cut from the bodies themselves.

- `tools/models/import_ubc.mjs` packs the bodies and hairstyles into GLBs (catalog ids 359-368).
- `tools/murim/assets/make_ubc_clothes.mjs` cuts a top, trousers, boots, sash and long skirt per body from its own mesh (ids 369-378), so they fit
  and follow every clip. A character on a base body that wears the old gear ids (214 body, 216 trousers, 217 boots, 218 sash, 354 skirt, 356-358 hair)
  gets the matching pieces (`ubcOutfit` in `app/scriptHost.ts`), so scripts did not change.
- `tools/models/extract_clips.mjs` + `ual2_clips.glb`: 42 clips from Universal Animation Library 2 (same skeleton), merged into the clip library as
  `ual_*`. The blade stance's light chain uses its sword swings.
- The mirror's hair choices are the new hairstyles (parted, long, buns, scholar's cap, shaven).
- Fighters keep the body's own skin and face; only spirits and shades are one flat colour (`tint`).

## World and rendering

- **Terrain detail layers** (`terrainMesh.ts`): grass, rock, sand and snow textures (ambientCG, CC0, 512 px colour and normal maps in `assets/source/kit/terrain/`, resized by `tools/models/downscale_textures.cjs`) are
  blended by per-vertex weights (`splat`) in world space at two scales so the repeat does not show. The palette stays the scene's (vertex colours); each layer multiplies over its own average colour.
- **Wind**: scattered plants (trees, bushes, grass, bamboo...) sway with a vertex shader; the tip moves most and each instance has its own phase (`wind` in `terrainMesh.ts`, advanced in `app/frame.ts`).
- **Frame governor** gains an `ao` flag (ambient occlusion turns off from tier 3). Azure Cloud Sect now uses SMAA and High shadows; ambient occlusion stays off for it, because GTAO re-draws the scene for normals and doubled its draw calls (688).
- NPCs stand in Universal Animation Library 2 idles (folded arms, leaning, lantern) instead of the default idle.

## Animation, depth effects and decals

- **Foot IK** (`footIK.ts`, tests in `tests/footIK.test.ts`): characters within 30 m stand on the terrain. The body drops to the lower foot and each leg is solved with
  two-bone IK, the knee bending the way the clip bent it. The terrain's height comes from `C.groundAt` (bilinear over the mesh's own heights).
- **Cloth and hair motion** (`clothSway.ts`): skirts, outer robes, long hair and buns lag behind a character's movement and ripple a little at rest, in a vertex shader on the worn piece.
- **Hit reactions**: heavy hits alternate between two knock-back clips (the second from Universal Animation Library 2).
- **Decals** (`decals.ts`): cracks and scorch under a finisher, dust where a body lands; a 24-quad pool, faded and reused oldest first.
- **Light shafts and contact shadows** (`depthFx.ts`): `PostProcessing.lightShafts` and `PostProcessing.contactShadows` (0-1). A full-screen pass reads the scene's depth; the frame governor turns it off from tier 3.
  Azure Cloud Sect uses 0.5 and 0.7.
- The example scenes were regenerated (the new `PostProcessing` fields serialise).

The engine already had hit stop, camera shake, slow motion on parries and finishers, impact sparks, rings and procedural sounds (`combatView.ts`); they are unchanged.

## Real cloth

- **Simulated skirts** (`cloth.ts`, `tests/cloth.test.ts`): the long skirt of a base body is a grid of particles pinned to the pelvis, stepped with Verlet integration, held by
  distance constraints, kept out of the legs by capsules and given more air drag toward the hem. It lags behind a run, swings forward on a stop, is shoved aside by a kick and hangs still at rest, in every clip,
  for the Player, NPCs, enemies and allies alike (it only needs the skeleton). Characters within 35 m are stepped; a teleport resets it. The Player now starts in the long robe cut, and the
  swordsman, adepts and the Iron Fist Elder wear skirts too.

## Surfaces, water and life

- **Surface detail** (`surfaceDetail.ts`): the wuxia kit's flat vertex colours get plaster, lacquered wood, roof tile and rock grain (ambientCG, CC0, `kit/surfaces/`), in world space, the material picked from the colour (leaves and gold are left alone).
- **Water** (`water.ts`): `Material.texture` `builtin:water` gives a surface with rippling normals and a Fresnel sky tint; the lake uses it.
- **Ambient life**: blossom petals over the courtyard, pollen by the village, fireflies at the lake, dust in the hall (world-space particles).
- **People differ in height**: Hua is tall, Mu and Wen shorter, Lan shorter again.

## Sound

- **Recorded impacts** (`sampleBank.ts`): Kenney's Impact Sounds (CC0) are layered over the synthesized combat voices: punches (light and heavy), plate hits for blocks, a bell for parries, soft falls, wooden crates and footsteps on grass and stone. They load in the background; until then the synthesized voice plays alone.
- **Recorded music** (`music.ts`): four CC0 tracks (OpenGameArt) as moods `wuxia-title`, `wuxia-explore`, `wuxia-night` and `wuxia-tension`, looped and faded; the generator stays quiet while one plays. Azure Cloud Sect uses them for exploring, night and danger.
  The loop seams of the recordings were not checked by ear.
