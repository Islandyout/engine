# F100: base characters and a wider clip library

Azure Cloud Sect's people were the engine's bare mannequin in clothes: no face, no hair, flat white skin. They are now Quaternius's CC0 Universal Base
Characters (a textured male and female body with eyes and eyebrows), with hairstyles rigged to the head bone and clothes cut from the bodies themselves.

- `tools/models/import_ubc.mjs` packs the bodies and hairstyles into GLBs (catalog ids 359-368).
- `tools/murim/assets/make_ubc_clothes.mjs` cuts a top, trousers, boots, sash and long skirt per body from its own mesh (ids 369-378), so they fit
  and follow every clip. A character on a base body that wears the old gear ids (214 body, 216 trousers, 217 boots, 218 sash, 354 skirt, 356-358 hair)
  gets the matching pieces (`ubcOutfit` in `app/scriptHost.ts`), so scripts did not change.
- `tools/models/extract_clips.mjs` + `ual2_clips.glb`: 42 clips from Universal Animation Library 2 (same skeleton), merged into the clip library as
  `ual_*`. The blade stance's light chain uses its sword swings.
- The mirror's hair choices are the new hairstyles (parted, long, buns, scholar's cap, shaven).
- Fighters keep the body's own skin and face; only spirits and shades are one flat colour (`tint`).

## World and rendering

- **Terrain detail layers** (`terrainMesh.ts`): grass, rock, sand and snow textures (ambientCG, CC0, 512 px colour and normal maps in `assets/source/kit/terrain/`, resized by `tools/models/downscale_textures.cjs`) are
  blended by per-vertex weights (`splat`) in world space at two scales so the repeat does not show. The palette stays the scene's (vertex colours); each layer multiplies over its own average colour.
- **Wind**: scattered plants (trees, bushes, grass, bamboo...) sway with a vertex shader; the tip moves most and each instance has its own phase (`wind` in `terrainMesh.ts`, advanced in `app/frame.ts`).
- **Frame governor** gains an `ao` flag (ambient occlusion turns off from tier 3). Azure Cloud Sect now uses SMAA and High shadows; ambient occlusion stays off for it, because GTAO re-draws the scene for normals and doubled its draw calls (688).
- NPCs stand in Universal Animation Library 2 idles (folded arms, leaning, lantern) instead of the default idle.
