# F112 Eden: dogs

- A stray dog (two in the east woods, one in the old orchard; the Creator can add more: "a dog") can be won over: someone with food who knows of it goes to it and feeds it; after three feedings it chooses them, and the journal says so.
- A dog that has someone follows them, steadies them (a person with their dog at their side is harder to frighten) and keeps them company (loneliness grows slower). A fox or wolf near such a dog turns away.
- The Minds tab and panel name dogs; the scene has a Dog prefab (the kit's shiba inu).

Not yet: dogs that hunt, bark a warning, breed or grieve; cattle and horses that can be kept.
