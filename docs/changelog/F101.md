# F101: Jianghu Road, an open-world murim game built from data

A second murim game, started from a fresh design (`docs/JIANGHU_ROAD_DESIGN.md`) instead of extending the Azure valley: a one-kilometre map with a walled city in
the middle, regions of rising danger round it, roads and waystones between them, and hand-made quests that cannot be lost.

- `tools/jianghu/world.ts` is the world as data (regions with danger tiers, hubs, roads, people, fighters, things); `tools/jianghu/quests.ts` is every quest as
  named steps (talk, kill, duel, reach, interact, meditate, have). `tools/jianghu/lint.ts` and `apps/editor/tests/jianghuQuests.test.ts` check that each step can be
  done, names something that exists, and that every item a quest asks for has a source, so no quest can be lost.
- `tools/jianghu/build_jianghu.ts` (`npm run jianghu`) writes `games/my-games/jianghu-road`: a 1000 m heightfield shaped per region (a peak, a plateau, a gorge, a
  basin, a lake, a mountain rim) with level ground for the city and each hub, the city and hubs, every person and fighter, and the systems that run them.
- Strength is fixed by who a fighter is and where they stand (`lua/bestiary.lua`); entering a region says how dangerous it is for your realm, and a target frame
  reads Trivial to Hopeless against yours. Nothing scales to the player. Strong people can be talked to or challenged to a duel to first yield.
- Quests (`lua/quests.lua`) track one waypoint at a time: only the tracked quest shows a marker, and Enter cycles through the active quests and none.
  A quest's state is saved, and a step names how to recover if it cannot be done as it stands.
- `lua/world.lua`: a day and weather cycle, waystones to find and ride between (10 silver), a place to rest. `lua/regions.lua`: region banners.
- Browser test `tests/browser/jianghu.cjs`: the mirror, the gatekeeper's quest, one tracked waypoint, Enter stops tracking.

Not done yet: content for the outer regions beyond one fighter each, roaming encounters, the later realms' quests and more hidden legendary items.
