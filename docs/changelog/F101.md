# F101: Jianghu Road, an open-world murim game built from data

A second murim game, started from a fresh design (`docs/JIANGHU_ROAD_DESIGN.md`) instead of extending the Azure valley: a one-kilometre map with a walled city in
the middle, regions of rising danger round it, roads and waystones between them, and hand-made quests that cannot be lost.

- `tools/jianghu/world.ts` is the world as data (regions with danger tiers, hubs, roads, people, fighters, things); `tools/jianghu/quests.ts` is every quest as
  named steps (talk, kill, duel, reach, interact, meditate, have). `tools/jianghu/lint.ts` and `apps/editor/tests/jianghuQuests.test.ts` check that each step can be
  done, names something that exists, and that every item a quest asks for has a source, so no quest can be lost.
- `tools/jianghu/build_jianghu.ts` (`npm run jianghu`) writes `games/my-games/jianghu-road`: a 1000 m heightfield shaped per region (a peak, a plateau, a gorge, a
  basin, a lake, a mountain rim) with level ground for the city and each hub, the city and hubs, every person and fighter, and the systems that run them.
- Strength is fixed by who a fighter is and where they stand (`lua/bestiary.lua`); entering a region says how dangerous it is for your realm, and a target frame
  reads Trivial to Hopeless against yours. Nothing scales to the player. Strong people can be talked to or challenged to a duel to first yield.
- Quests (`lua/quests.lua`) track one waypoint at a time: only the tracked quest shows a marker, and Enter cycles through the active quests and none.
  A quest's state is saved, and a step names how to recover if it cannot be done as it stands.
- `lua/world.lua`: a day and weather cycle, waystones to find and ride between (10 silver), a place to rest. `lua/regions.lua`: region banners.
- Browser test `tests/browser/jianghu.cjs`: the mirror, the gatekeeper's quest, one tracked waypoint, Enter stops tracking.

## Realistic nature

- **Rocks**: five Poly Haven photoscans (CC0) simplified to 500-1500 triangles with meshoptimizer (`tools/models/import_rocks.mjs`, catalog ids 399-403); the colour and normal maps carry the detail.
- **Grass** (`tools/models/make_grass.mjs`, ids 404-406): tufts of real curved blades (110-130 triangles), not cards. A scatter rule with `near=M` (`grassField.ts`) is not placed in the terrain:
  its tufts are grown on the ground within M metres of the camera, a few to the square metre, and regrown as the camera moves, so a kilometre of land is grassy where you stand for a few thousand instances.
- **Trees**: Jianghu Road scatters the EZ-Tree trees and bushes (ids 255-261) in groves; the low-poly wuxia trees are no longer used there.
- **Far versions per instance** (`scatterLod.ts`): a terrain's scatter chose near or far versions per chunk, and a sparse model's chunk can be hundreds of metres wide, so one tree near the camera drew every tree in its chunk in full.
  Each instance now draws as the detailed model within 70 m and as its far version beyond, and is dropped beyond 320 m. Jianghu Road went from 15.5 to 3.3 million triangles (`tests/browser/jianghu_perf.cjs`, budget 4 million).
- A terrain over 700 m a side places up to one scatter instance per 60 m² (the cap was 4000 instances for any size).
- **Roads follow the ground** (`Terrain.paths`, `terrainPaths.ts`): roads and the city's paved streets are painted into the terrain's colour and detail layer (`dirt 5 x z x z ...`, `stone 8 ...`), so they hug every slope.
  Slabs laid on the ground left gaps under their downhill edges; they are gone, and so are 450 entities.
- **Buildings and walls stand on their lowest ground** (`footGround` in the builder): a model's footprint is sampled and it is placed at the lowest height under it, so the uphill side sits in the hill and there is no gap under the downhill side.
- Clothes are lighter (they read as black on the base bodies' textures).

## Running fast in the browser

Researched (three.js performance guides, KTX2/Basis, WebGL grass write-ups) and applied what fits this engine:

- **Download size**: `tools/models/shrink_glb_images.cjs` re-encodes the images inside GLBs (JPEG when opaque, PNG when not) at 1024 px for characters and hair, 512 px for trees. The base bodies went from 8 MB to 1 MB each;
  Jianghu Road's first load is 20 MB instead of 34 MB, and the GPU holds a hundred megabytes fewer of uncompressed texture. (KTX2/Basis would shrink GPU memory further but needs `toktx`, which this repository does not carry.)
- **Per-instance far versions** (`scatterLod.ts`), cull distance 320 m, no shadows from far versions or grass: 15.5 to 3.3 million triangles.
- **Grass** (`grassField.ts`): each cell's matrices are built once and cached; moving only copies arrays, and the fade at the edge of reach is in the vertex shader (no per-frame JavaScript over every tuft).
- **Frame governor**: its scatter fraction (it drops tiers when frames run slow) now thins terrain scatter and grass; before, it only reached space scatter.
- A loading bar while a player build downloads its models (in the exported page from the first paint, `tools/export_build.mjs`; main.ts fills it and removes it when play starts).

## Legs

The foot IK solver turned the leg bones further every frame while a held (paused) pose was not rewriting them, so on a slope a leg drifted out over a few seconds. It now remembers the pose it started
from and starts from that again while the animation has not rewritten the bone (`footIK.ts`, tested in `tests/footIK.test.ts`).

## Content pass

- **World**: water in the lake and Old Lotus' pond, camps with fires at the Black Wind lookout, the smugglers' reeds and the fortress yard, the fortress gate and banners, boulders round the outer hubs, bone spires, wrecked carts and a cracked arch in the Valley of Bones (ash-coloured ground, no trees), Old Lotus' hut and the Port Jade pier, a filled Cloudgate (townhouses, huts, oaks, a well, the furnace, lanterns), gentler and noisier mountains round the map edge, painted yards at every hub (`Terrain.paths` kinds: dirt, gravel, sand, ash, stone).
- **People**: ten townsfolk (a blacksmith and an alchemist who trade, a baker, a student, a night watchman, a storyteller, a child, a fisher, a dockhand, the shrine keeper); three strangers on the roads who duel to first yield and give something (Drunken Master Lu, Wandering Nun Ji, Mountain Hermit Gu), a dire wolf, and fighters in every outer region (Peak acolytes, Blood Lotus disciples, Frostfang guards, Bone Wraiths).
- **Quests**: five more, each linted (`The Guardian's Fists`, `Wei Remembers`, `The Black Wind's Ledger`, `What Sleeps in the Valley`, `The Last Manual`), with their manuals (Iron Hide, Lotus Step, Keen Eye, Warlord's Hide, Nine-Dragon Breath) and items (Guardian's Wraps, Blood Lotus Seal, Frostfang Blade, Warlord's Seal, Nine Dragon Manual), and waystones at the gorge, the fortress and the bones. Realms 5 to 7 now have somewhere to go and something to find.
- Clothes are lifted toward white by realm of fighter; crates and barrels are smaller.

## Gaps pass

From a pass over reviews of Where Winds Meet and Wandering Sword and open-world design write-ups (performance, curiosity rewarded early, resources tied to landmarks, progressive map discovery, early tutorials forgotten):

- **A world map** (M), which the key hints promised and nothing drew: regions you have been to in their danger colour with their name, the rest as mist with a "?", every road, the hubs of known regions and every waystone you have found (`regions.lua`, `world.lua`).
- **Engine: maps for worlds whose north is -z.** The compass reads -z as north, the full map drew +z at the top, so in Jianghu Road the map was upside down against the compass. `hud.map_layout` takes `north -z`: the layout image turns half round and the north-up minimap with it (`minimap.ts`, tested).
- **Small finds every half-minute of walking**: sixteen wayside searches (a broken cart, a roadside shrine, a stele, an ore outcrop) with silver, herbs, pills, ore or arrows, each by a landmark you can see from the road.
- **Each region its own growth** (`in=X,Z,R` scatter option, `foliage.ts`, tested): bamboo groves in the Whispering Bamboo Forest, gold-leafed trees in Crimson Maple Gorge, pines and boulders on Frostfang Ridge and Heaven Peak, ash and bushes in the marches.
- **Realistic bamboo** (`tools/models/make_bamboo.mjs`, catalog id 407): a stand of tapering, noded culms with leaf sprays, with a far version; it replaces the low-poly bamboo clumps.
- **The summit path**: Nine-Dragon Summit was a cone too steep to climb. A switchback path is cut into the mountain from the shrine to the top, rising evenly, and painted as gravel.
- **Fight poses** (`fightPoses.ts`, tested): the held block is a high guard (forearms up in front of the face, chin tucked) built on each fighter's own skeleton from its guard; the library's block twisted, crossed the wrists and ended in a wide crouch with the arms apart. Kicks keep the guard's hands up instead of flinging both arms forward.
- **Mana while talking**: the arts on the number keys are locked during a conversation (and at the mirror) instead of the mana being set aside, so the mana bar no longer reads empty in every dialogue.
- **A floating head at load**: a base body under clothes still downloading was cut for those clothes, so for the first seconds every character was a head on the ground. The body is now only cut under pieces already loaded.

Not done yet: content for the outer regions beyond one fighter each, roaming encounters, the later realms' quests and more hidden legendary items.
