# F147: Eden phase J, thinking and problem solving

`wisdom.ts` and `tech.ts` (hooked into `mind.ts` by one intent kind, `work`, one `techOptions` call and one `techStep` call).

**J1 causes, analogies, method, being wrong.** Six causes (`CAUSES`): a roof keeps the cold off, getting wet in the open chills, dirt brings sickness, rough water capsizes boats, cooked food fills more, fire keeps beasts off (the F135 one, now also revisable). Each is a rule in `person.rules` fed by observation (`watchCauses`, one episode per two minutes, or `witness` from a boat trip or a meal). Two hits and a belief of 0.62 make it an idea they hold and can tell (`exchangeIdeas` passes it on; a told belief starts at "taken on trust"). The ones about cold and dirt change what they do (go to a shelter, wash). Five or more tries with belief under 0.38 and they drop it, note "I was wrong", feel regret, and the belief goes back to doubtful. `ANALOGIES` (10 pairs, e.g. weir to pen, plank to bridge) add 0.25 to the chance of hitting on the new thing and are named in the journal. A method (0 to 0.6) grows with each finding and is shown to people nearby who trust the teacher; it adds up to 0.4 to the chance of hitting on things.

**J3 inventions.** `CRAFTS`: 45 new, plus the 3 of F135 = 48 (`inventionCount()`, `inventionsFound(world)`). Each has `needs` (ideas that must be known first), a cost (wood, seed, or gathered stone, clay, fibre, ore, copper, bronze, iron), a site, and what it makes. Chain depth to iron is 8. A curious person who knows the prior ideas and has the stuff tries it and may hit on it (hash chance, not their random stream); failing spoils a piece. A 12-day run of three curious people found 18 by themselves, in order.

**J4 farming and the rest.** `farming` makes a field (food thing that regrows); hoe, plough and irrigation raise its capacity, tending speeds growth; 8 days of three farmers stood on 62 food against 41 for the same people without it. Feeding a wild cow, donkey, alpaca or horse three times makes it theirs and teaches `herding`; kept animals drift to their pen or owner. Kiln, ore, copper, bronze, bellows, iron, axes (wood 1.5x and 2x). Wheel, cart (loads of 8 wood and 16 capacity), yoke, potter's wheel (a pot feeds six).

**J5 boats and bridges.** Raft, boat, oar, sail, plank, bridge. Someone with a boat or a finished bridge crosses a lake (`world.noGo`) when walking round is at least 8 m longer, to food or home at dusk (`crossingJob`). While crossing they are exempt from `keepOut`. Boats can capsize (more in rough weather); a timid person will not use an unfamiliar way across until they have seen others use it (`riskOf`); two capsizings in rough weather teach the cause and they then reckon the weather in. A boat is also rowed out to fish.

**J2 shared plans, risk.** `world.projects`: a bridge (one planks per 5 m of span, laid by whoever brings wood) and a granary (10 wood, 12 food, a larder for the whole camp). One who knows how proposes it, others join by sociability and trust. In the test four people finished the granary over more than a season. Risk is as in J5, also for taking a first step on the unfamiliar.

**J6 objects.** Built by a `work` intent and used: pot, spit, oven (cooking feeds those near), loom (cloth), quern, ball, swing, flute, drum (play), well (drink), hive (honey), field (tend), boat (fish), plus kiln (smelting), pen, channel, dog-bed, raft, bridge.

**NOT done, said plainly.**

They exist in the mind only. Not seen in the running game; the lake there is 52 m across, so a bridge there is 11 planks and is not yet tried live; the host's body movement over water (`externalMotion`) is not handled.
- `AFFORDANCES` has plain entries for the new kinds; no per-kind sounds or animations. `ideaName` shows craft names; the causes show as their ids.
- Effects that are only a step in a chain, with no use of their own: basket, spindle, stone knife, digging stick, rope, fish hook, sickle (a little), oar (a little), sail (speed), wheel.
- A granary is built once and not refilled yearly. Dogs do not sleep in a dog bed; the bed only sends the dog there. Animals are tamed, not bred, ridden or yoked. Bow gives the hunting flag only. Metals feed axes only; no coins, no armour.
- Rules for causes are not shared as "teaching how to solve" beyond the method number.
- Not viewed in the browser. The model-planner (llmPlan) does not know the new crafts.
