# F127 Eden: talk to anyone, and the last missing motions

- **E beside someone** (while you walk the world) opens a box to type to them. If more than one person is within 6 m you get a list to choose from (click, or press 1-9); "Someone else" goes back to the list; Esc or Leave closes it. A small "E talk to Awan" hint shows when someone is near. The game does not hear the keys typed in the box.
- They answer from who they are (`eden/chat.ts`): greetings by the hour and by how well they know you, how they feel, where they have seen water, food, shelter, fire or danger (a direction and a distance, only from what they remember), what they think of another person, a story or what is on their mind, what they want, what they need and how you can help, thanks, sorry, goodbye. Someone with food and a warm nature or some trust will spare you a meal (it heals you). Being talked to is company, is written in their journal, builds a trust in you, and keeps them standing to listen. With the free voice on, the answer is put in their own words.
- New motions: carrying food or wood, people walk with the carry-walk clip; talking has a hand gesture and head movement; a wave pose for greetings. All the earlier poses carry over.
- Fixed: after clicking "Walk the world" the button kept the keyboard focus and swallowed W A S D; it is released now, and E and H are heard whichever element has focus.
- Not yet done: a real lying-down clip (the pose layer lies them down), people facing you while you talk, voice input.
