# F111 Eden: weather with a sky, wounds and nursing

- Weather (`earth.ts`): wind that gusts and turns, stronger in autumn and winter and with rain; rain that is a drizzle or a downpour; mist at dawn after a wet night and low cloud in the cold months; snow when precipitation falls below 1 °C. The sky follows it (rain strength, mist and wind direction are sent to the scene) and the panel header names it (drizzle, heavy rain, snow, mist, windy). Wind takes warmth away from anyone out of shelter.
- Wounds: being caught by an animal leaves a wound that mends with rest and food, drains a little strength, and can turn bad (an illness) if the person is hungry or cold. Sitting with the sick or wounded is nursing: it eases them and teaches the nurse the new skill of healing (role "healer"). People who care for someone ill or hurt are drawn to them as for someone low.
