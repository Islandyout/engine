# F98 — The wuxia kit, and Azure Cloud Sect rebuilt on it (0.95.0)

- **Wuxia kit**: 15 original low-poly models (pagoda hall and tiers, spire, paifang gate, pavilion, lanterns, garden wall, moon gate, arched bridge, training dummy, weapon rack, incense burner, stele) generated by `tools/murim/assets/make_wuxia_kit.mjs`, in the catalog as category `wuxia`, ids 313-327. No third-party art.
- **Azure Cloud Sect** uses them for the sect compound and the shrine instead of boxes, and dresses the player and the elder in robes.
- **Reference tool**: `tools/ui_refs/grab.py` fetches game UI reference screenshots as contact sheets.
- **Assessment**: [AZURE_CLOUD_SECT.md](../AZURE_CLOUD_SECT.md) rates the game area by area, with sources, and sets the roadmap.
- **World**: Heaven Peak and two crags as landmarks; a roadside shrine, a ruined watchtower and a hermit's hut along the way; four spirit veins (a stele ringed with light) where meditation is three times faster.
- **Performance** ([AZURE_CLOUD_SECT.md](../AZURE_CLOUD_SECT.md)): the scene went from 5.2 million triangles to 0.96 million. Six low-poly trees in the wuxia kit, one primitive and one draw call per kit model (vertex colours), fewer lights, a throttled script HUD. A browser test holds the budget.
- **Enemies**: five bandits of four kinds (brawler, swordsman, spearman, two qi adepts) with their own move lists; the target frame names the kind. **Hit flash**: a struck fighter goes white for about 0.09 s, scaled by the Impact flashes setting (`CombatView.flashStrength`).
- **Cultivation**: four stages in each realm and a tribulation (lightning with telegraphed rings) at Foundation and Golden Core.
- **Willow Village**: a merchant (silver, elixirs, pills), Wen and a side quest, a Dialogue widget conversation driven from Lua, silver dropped by bandits.
