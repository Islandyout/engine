# F133 Eden: birds, leaping fish, music, snow on the ground, inheritance

**Birds and fish.** `edenWildlife.ts`: nine birds (ellipsoid body, beak, flapping wings, glides between bursts) circle about you by day, fewer in rain and winter; a fish now and then leaps from the river or lake near you with a splash ring. They are drawn from three.js primitives, so there is no model to license (no verifiably free animated bird exists in the packs we could reach; the three.js example birds have no stated licence, so they are not used). Scenery only: the minds never see them.

**Music.** `edenSound.ts` plays a slow tune that follows the season (bright pentatonic in spring and summer, minor in autumn and winter) with a held chord now and then, sparser at night and in rain. N turns all sound off, music included.

**Snow cover.** While it snows the terrain whitens toward snow; it melts when the air is above 2 °C. Cleared when you stop playing.

**Inheritance.** When someone dies, what they carried (wood, food, seed, hides) and any keepsake they were carving pass to their eldest grown child, else their partner, else whoever loved them most; it is written in the heir's journal and the chronicle.

**Speed.** Mind step cost: 6 people + 20 creatures 0.23 ms, 30 + 80 1.3 ms, 60 + 200 4.6 ms (the page steps about every 100 ms).
