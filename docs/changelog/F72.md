# F72 — Pale Signal slice 2: walking anywhere, the scanner, seas and clouds, and the Kestrel (0.72.0)

The second pass against the [Pale Signal](https://github.com/Islandyout/pale-signal) prototype brings in its exploration loop: land and walk anywhere, scan to learn, talk to the Talari, and upgrade the ship with what you learn. The slice is documented in [docs/space/PALE_SIGNAL_SLICE.md](space/PALE_SIGNAL_SLICE.md).

## Engine

- **Walking anywhere**:
  - **Re-anchoring**: after a touchdown outside the authored site, the bridge moves the walk/draw frame to the landing point (`Runtime::anchor_frame`). It re-samples the walkable heightfield from that body and parks the site's colliders, making them triggers.
  - **Returning**: landing back inside the site restores the home frame, its saved heightfield and its colliders. `space.place_landed` reframes too.
  - **Signals**: events `frame:<body>` and `frame:home`; `editor_space_value` 37 (away), 38 (frame generation) and 39 (frame body); `space.state().frame` and `.away`.
  - **The editor**: rebuilds the ground surface on a frame change and hides the site's authored objects while away.
- **Seas**: `Body::sea_level`. `surface_height` counts water as surface (ships land on it, walkers stand on it); `terrain_height` is the seabed, which the editor meshes and tints by depth. Staged with `editor_space_body_sea`; `editor_space_wet(x, z)` reports water in the walk frame.
- **Ship tuning from scripts**: `space.tune(field, value)` (thrust, lift, fuel, hull, heat, rcs, land_vertical, land_slope) and `space.spec()`.
- **`Scannable`** (id, name, kind Flora/Fauna/Mineral/Culture/Landmark, range) and **the scanner** (`scanner.ts`):
  - **Targets**: Scannable entities, scattered species and landmarks.
  - **Scanning**: hold F facing a target. Aim and stillness speed the scan; switching targets keeps a little progress.
  - **Results**: each key completes once (one specimen catalogues its species) and calls `on_ui("scan", key)` in every script.
  - **HUD**: a reticle with a progress ring and catalogued state, plus a text line for tests.
- **Body options** in `SpaceSystem.bodies` (`key=value` after the colours): `sea`, `clouds`, `snow`. **`SpaceSystem.species`**: id, body, class, catalog model, weight, scale, name and description.
- **`Spaceship.model = "Kestrel"`**: the built-in survey ship replaces the authored look. Engine flames sit on its nacelles.
- **Play mode**: clicking the world no longer selects entities (Edit only).

## Visuals (`spaceView.ts`, `spaceArt.ts`)

- **Terrain**: seas flattened to the surface, tinted shallow to deep, with low roughness through a `water` vertex attribute. Sand at the shore, snow on high ground on snowy worlds, and per-vertex variation.
- **Cloud layers**: seamless fBm coverage on a sphere, drifting.
- **Sky**: sunsets put an orange horizon band toward a low sun and warm the sunlight. The Milky Way is a dense band of faint stars with nebulae; both fade with daylight.
- **Landmark structures** you can walk up to: a glyph-lit monolith ringed by kneeling dishes. Their beams fade as you arrive.
- **Plant and rock scatter** around any walk frame, from the body's species and deterministic per frame. It stays clear of the ship or site and of water.
- **Chase camera**: out of the air it rises and looks down past the ship, so the world stays in frame.

## The slice

The generator adds:
- **Species**: the prototype's catalogue.
- **The Talari**: named residents with the prototype's lines.
- **Evidence**: three Culture scannables.
- **Landmarks**: the Kneeling Array, the Under-Ice Relay and the Anvil.
- **Other scannables**: the Black Foundation and the volatile ice.
- **New UI**: research, language, oxygen, dialogue and upgrades.

The director adds:
- research points;
- the prototype's upgrade costs, applied through `space.tune`;
- the language model with its translation stages;
- suit oxygen on airless worlds;
- marker handling across frames;
- the walk-up ending at the relay.

Tests: walking anywhere in `tests/editor_bridge_tests.cpp`, sea level in `tests/space_tests.cpp`, species and options in `apps/editor/tests/paleSignal.test.ts`, and the scanner and Vell in `tests/browser/pale_signal.cjs`.
