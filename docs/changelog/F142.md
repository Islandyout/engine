# F142: Eden phase E, ownership, markets, money, debt, capital, labour

`economy.ts`. **Ownership:** what a person builds or plants (shelter, crop, snare, weir) carries an `owner`; eating from, or stealing from, another's thing is a grievance with the owner (trust and affinity fall, anger; the starving are less blamed; the owner's kin may use it); where two or more people saw it, or an account is kept, restitution (1.5 times) becomes a debt. Owned things of others are avoided unless hunger is past 0.85. At death land goes to the eldest heir, coin is shared, and debts pass on. **Specialisation:** a person's best skill (at least 0.3) is their speciality; what they make is counted by good; output per day is skill, raised by a spear or net; the fair carries surplus (food over 3, wood over 4, hide over 1) to those who want it. **Trade:** a fair on every fourth day at the middle of the camp; prices (in food units) follow demand over supply each day and are kept as a series; before counting a price is a feeling and an amount a handful (+-30%), after it both are exact; haggling is set by boldness, patience and trust and stays within 80-120% of the going price; a caravan from far away comes every few days (twice as often once a boat is built) and buys surplus for shells and hide; a middleman (`broker`) buys of one and sells to another for a profit; food spoils (4%, 8%, 5%, 1% a day by season; a third as fast in a shelter, a seventh in a granary), wood, hide and shells do not. **Money:** a durable good taken in payment, without being wanted, by at least three different people and six times is adopted as money (the chronicle says so) and prices are then quoted in it; shells turn up now and then at the water. **Debt:** loans carry compound interest (25% a year, more for the mistrusted and the poor; none before counting), are repaid at the term, default after four more days (trust falls, no more credit from that lender), and an unpaid debt becomes bonded work until the labour clears it. **Capital:** a day's making beyond a day's keep is saved and earns 0.4% a day once above 3; a shortfall eats it ("the seed is eaten"); a Gini over wealth and a class (owner, commoner, worker, poor, bonded) are kept. A venture (boat, mill) is funded in wood for shares, built when funded, pays food by share each day; speculation pushes a share above its worth and past a threshold it crashes. A granary held in common (dues from members, tribute from `levy`) pays a member who is hungry with nothing. **Labour:** `hire` (a day's wage in a good), the worker's output goes to the employer, unpaid twice and the worker leaves; `command` lets an employer or bonded-labour creditor order a task, which adds to the worker's score unless hunger or thirst is high. **Institutions:** with no counting nothing is recorded and a due debt is forgotten and disputed one time in four; with counting (tally) or writing the ledger records every loan, repayment, sale and wage and `ledgerBalance` reads what is owed; with an account kept a default is known to all lenders.

Hooks in `mind.ts` are small: `economyAfter` in `finish`, `economyTheft` in the steal case, `marketTrade` at the top of the trade case, `economyWorldStep` and `economyStep` in `step`, `economyBias` beside the other biases, `inheritHoldings` in `inherit`. State is `Person.econ`, `Thing.owner`, `World.econ` (saved by `persist.ts`). Chances come from a hash, not the person's random stream.

**Not done, said plainly.** The old food/wood swap still uses the fixed `tradeRates`; market prices apply only at the fair. No coinage (there are no metals; shells are found, not minted). Tribute and the granary tax are set by a call (`levy`, `WorldEcon.tribute`), not by any leader (Phase G). Ventures, hiring, lending to the poor beyond the neighbour rule, and brokering are functions the tests call: nobody starts a boat, hires a worker or becomes a merchant on their own. No chattel slavery: bonded labour for debt is the only form, and the rest (captives, coercion) belongs to Phase H. Savings are a number (`capital`), not goods in a store. The fair, prices, and caravan are not drawn in the editor. Property law is only the restitution debt; there are no judges (Phase G). Land is things with an owner, with no boundaries or parcels. Debts of the dead are inherited, not settled from the estate. The Gini rise is shown in a test with fixed shocks, not measured in the sweep, where lives are four days.
