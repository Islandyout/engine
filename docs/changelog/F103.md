# F103 Eden

`games/my-games/eden`: Adam and Eve, two people who live their own lives in a garden (river, fruit grove, meadow, overlook, resting hollow, two landmark trees). Everything runs on the player's machine: no network, no account, no AI model.

- Their minds are plain code (`apps/editor/src/eden/mind.ts`, tested in `tests/edenMind.test.ts`): needs that grow with time (hunger, thirst, tiredness, loneliness, joy), a personality that weights what they care about, a memory of what they have seen and how it felt, fear that overrides everything, and seeded chance, so the same seed gives the same life and different people choose differently.
- `apps/editor/src/editor/app/edenAgents.ts` runs the minds while an Eden scene plays, tells each person's Lua script (`tools/eden/lua/{adam,eve}.lua`) where to walk and how fast, and shows what each is doing in a small panel. The scripts walk straight at a target when the path grid has no route.
- The panel lets the player say "let there be..." to add a Deer, Fox, Alpaca, Oak Tree, Pine Tree, Bush or Rock; each becomes something the people notice (a fox is feared, a bush is eaten from). A leading article is ignored ("a deer").
- A camera follows Adam; drag to orbit.
- People drink from the river bank (`DRINK_SPOT` in `src/eden/world.ts`), not from the water.

Not yet: speech beyond short remarks, goals set by the people themselves, memory of who did what, more than two people, building and carrying things.

The earlier Supabase and Groq brain was removed from the game. The Supabase project it used still exists and still runs its cron job; nothing in the repo uses it.

## Lifelike check

`apps/editor/src/eden/lifelike.ts` runs the minds through simulated lives (`tools/eden/scenarios.ts`: 1200 lives of about a week each; `tests/edenLives.test.ts`: 200) with 1 to 4 people of random personalities, gardens, food that runs out, and a fox or fire, and measures them against what a person would do: staying alive, meals and drinks a day, one sleep a day at night, variety, meeting each other, not standing about, not changing their mind every few seconds. The first run had 39% of lives losing someone and 22 meals an hour; it now loses 0.25% and eats about three times a day. What it changed:

- A day is 24 sim minutes (3x on screen, so about 8 real minutes) with a clock; people sleep at night and the scene is lit for the hour.
- Needs pull only past a threshold (nobody eats when full), and food grows back.
- A person who does not know where water or food is goes looking, toward parts of the garden not yet visited, kept inside the garden's edge.
- A critical need clears the menu of everything but its remedy; desperation makes a person braver near a danger; pain makes anyone run.
- Wandering and choosing avoid known dangers.

## The Creator's tools

- **Saving**: the world (people with what they remember, feel, carry and have been through; things and what is left of them; the weather's hand) is saved to the browser every 20 s, when the page is hidden, on Save, and on every Creator command; a reload or a new launch carries on from it. New world asks twice. The desktop app's local server uses a fixed port so the saved world is found again.
- **Time**: pause, 1×, 3×, 10×, 30×, 100×. Up to 3× the bodies walk; above it the minds walk themselves and the bodies are put where they are.
- **Minds tab**: how each person is (needs, mood, health), what they weighed and chose, goals, how they feel about others, what they remember of the world, and their journal.
- **Creator tab**: plain words (`creator.ts`): create a thing ("let there be a deer by the river"), weather (rain, clear, warm, cold snap), season ("make it winter"), do for a person (heal, feed, quench, bless, hurt, wake, warm), move someone to a place, reshape a person ("make Eve bolder"), speak to them ("tell Adam well done"). Each says what it did or why it could not.
