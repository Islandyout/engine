# F103 Eden

`games/my-games/eden`: Adam and Eve, two people who live their own lives in a garden (river, fruit grove, meadow, overlook, resting hollow, two landmark trees). Everything runs on the player's machine: no network, no account, no AI model.

- Their minds are plain code (`apps/editor/src/eden/mind.ts`, tested in `tests/edenMind.test.ts`): needs that grow with time (hunger, thirst, tiredness, loneliness, joy), a personality that weights what they care about, a memory of what they have seen and how it felt, fear that overrides everything, and seeded chance, so the same seed gives the same life and different people choose differently.
- `apps/editor/src/editor/app/edenAgents.ts` runs the minds while an Eden scene plays, tells each person's Lua script (`tools/eden/lua/{adam,eve}.lua`) where to walk and how fast, and shows what each is doing in a small panel. The scripts walk straight at a target when the path grid has no route.
- The panel lets the player say "let there be..." to add a Deer, Fox, Alpaca, Oak Tree, Pine Tree, Bush or Rock; each becomes something the people notice (a fox is feared, a bush is eaten from). A leading article is ignored ("a deer").
- A camera follows Adam; drag to orbit.
- People drink from the river bank (`DRINK_SPOT` in `src/eden/world.ts`), not from the water.

Not yet: speech beyond short remarks, goals set by the people themselves, memory of who did what, more than two people, building and carrying things.

The earlier Supabase and Groq brain was removed from the game. The Supabase project it used still exists and still runs its cron job; nothing in the repo uses it.
