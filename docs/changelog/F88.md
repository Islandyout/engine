# F88 — One loadout, loot, and the UI kit (0.88.0)

Three additions:

- **One loadout**: guns and melee weapons share the same slots.
- **Loot**: pickups, enemy drops and crates.
- **The UI kit**: 21 ready-made game UI widgets in six styles. They replace the plain-text gun HUD, and the editor can add them as templates.

Guides: [SHOOTING.md](../SHOOTING.md) and [UI_KIT.md](../UI_KIT.md).

## One loadout

- **New slot keys**:
  - `melee=<name>` makes a `Weapons` slot a melee weapon (a `kit/weapons` name, or `fists`);
  - `offhand=<name>` gives a gun a blade in the other hand.
- **Switching**: 1–9, Q, the wheel and d-pad up go through guns and blades alike.
- **The fighter**:
  - A Player with a melee slot gets a `Melee` fighter automatically.
  - The fighter's moveset follows the blade: martial arts for fists, a dagger or a knife; the sword set otherwise.
- **Who does what**:
  - A melee slot never fires or reloads.
  - A plain gun slot just shoots.
  - A gun with `offhand=` strikes unaimed and shoots aimed (the F87 dagger-and-pistol fighter, now one slot).
- **Bad lines say why**: for example, "a melee slot (melee=) can't also hold a gun (held=)".

## Loot

- **The loot line** (`engine::gameplay::parse_loot`): `<chance> weapon <loadout line> | ammo N | health N | item Name [N]`.
  - Each line may carry `rarity=Common…Legendary`.
  - Every line rolls on its own.
  - An item name can be several words ("Range Key").
- **New components**:
  - **Pickup**: turns over a ring under a beam of its rarity colour, and respawns if set.
  - **LootDrop**: what an enemy drops on death, optionally its gun.
  - **Container**: a crate or chest with a press or hold time, and an optional key item it uses up.
- **Picking up**:
  - Walk over ammo, health and items.
  - Press E (pad Y) for weapons and anything marked `press`.
  - A weapon joins the loadout. With nine slots, it swaps with the gun in hand, which drops.
- **Opening**:
  - A lid (a `lid`/`top` node) swings back, or a lidless crate breaks.
  - The table spills out as pickups.
  - Sounds for pickups by rarity, crates and locked chests.
- **Items**:
  - The Player carries counts by name.
  - Lua: `world.items`, `world.give_item`, `world.take_item`.
- **Bridge**: `editor_set_pickup`, `editor_set_loot_drop`, `editor_set_container`, the interact target and loot events. Bad tables fail the scene with the line and the reason.

## The UI kit (`uiKit.ts`)

- **Widgets**: 21, in four groups.

  | Group | Widgets |
  | --- | --- |
  | Combat HUD | Health, Ammo, Crosshair, Damage numbers, Kill feed, Boss bar |
  | Navigation | Minimap, Compass, Objective, Waypoints |
  | Interaction & loot | Interact prompt, Pickup feed, Loot card, Inventory, Hotbar, Weapon wheel |
  | Menus & dialogue | Pause menu, Main menu, Settings, Dialogue, Notification |

- **Styles**: six, after shipped games.

  | Style | After |
  | --- | --- |
  | Modern | Apex Legends, Valorant |
  | Battle Royale | Fortnite |
  | Tactical | The Division, Call of Duty |
  | Fantasy | Elden Ring, Zelda |
  | Sci-fi | Destiny, Halo |
  | Minimal | — |

  Each style has its own type, plates, bars, key caps, icons, glow or outline.

- **The UI component**: a new `Widget` kind with `widget` and `style` fields. Older scenes load as before.
- **Live data**: the Player's health, gun, loadout, items, heading, waypoints, enemies, loot, hits, kills and a boss.
  - While editing, the widgets show sample data.
  - Authored text drives Objective, Boss bar, the menus, Dialogue (`Speaker: line`, `> choice`) and Notification; `ui.set_text` changes it.
- **Clicks**:
  - The Pause menu resumes, restarts, opens settings and quits.
  - The Main menu's Play hides it.
  - The Hotbar, Inventory and Weapon wheel switch weapons (`editor_select_weapon`).
  - The Settings sliders and toggles change the player settings.
  - Dialogue choices reach scripts as `on_ui(name, "choice:N")`.
- **By default**:
  - The plain-text crosshair, ammo and health of the gun HUD are now kit widgets.
  - Loot gets the interact prompt, the pickup feed and a loot card.
  - The style is the scene's first widget's, or Modern.
- **UI templates** (Project › UI templates…): each group's widgets previewed in all six styles. A click adds one; **Whole group** adds a group in one style.
- **Research**: [UI_KIT.md](../UI_KIT.md) lists six or more reference games per widget, with the sources the kit follows.

## The Combat range template

- **The loot corner**: a supply crate, a chest locked by the Range Key, a shotgun, ammo and a medkit.
- **Drops**: targets drop ammo, health and coins.
- **UI kit widgets**: minimap, compass, kill feed, damage numbers, hotbar and a pause menu.
