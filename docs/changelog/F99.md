# F99: Valley map for Azure Cloud Sect

- The engine's area map takes a game's own look: `path x z x z ...` (a winding road), `title kicker|name` and `legend kind text` lines in `hud.map_layout`, a `vein` icon for `hud.map_marker`. Without `title` and `legend` the Gate map is unchanged.
- Azure Cloud Sect sets its valley (sect, camp, shrine, village, peaks, road) and markers (elder, shrine, merchant, spirit veins). M opens it; the HUD's Minimap widget stays the small map.
- Meditation moved from M to N (M is the map).
- A Skill tree widget (script-fed nodes, links, click to learn) and new item icons (potion, pill, scroll, robe, bolt, flame; the Inventory picks them by item name). Azure Cloud Sect has six passive perks on K, paid with insight.
- Projectile looks: a move's `shape=` (0 orb, 1 crescent, 2 arrow, 3 fireball, 4 lightning, 5 shuriken) reaches the projectile and the editor draws it as glowing unlit parts instead of a box.
- Seven kit pieces (canvas tent, farmhouse, watchtower, thatched hut, roadside shrine, market stall, hand cart) replace the box tents, scaled halls and a lone roof used as a tower; the sect's west wall leaves room for the moon gate.
- Azure Cloud Sect: scroll arts (Instructor Hua, keys 5-7), Jade Dew Pill (Z), spirit herbs and an alchemy furnace, wayside shrine and altar (pray to rest, return and save), qinggong air jumps, day and night, weather, save and load. The gap list is in `docs/MURIM_GAPS.md`.
- A Key hints widget replaces the loose text line of menu keys under the hot bar: key caps and labels on one plate, in the HUD's style. Azure Cloud Sect's test now fails on any plain text in its HUD.
- Bodies: a person with no brain or controller (a villager, the elder) was stretched to its 0.6 m collision box across its T-pose, a third of its width; animated people now keep their proportions from the box's height like fighters do. A gear tint (`world.wear` "id:#rrggbb") is now the piece's colour: multiplied onto the kit's dark vertex colours, light robes came out black.
