# F136 Eden: new problems, parties, far-off trips, cause and effect, and an optional model as planner

Closes the gaps listed after F135.

**New problems.** `eden/solve.ts` `solveProblem`: a dangerous beast (danger 0.8 or more, a wolf) near camp is a problem to be worked out, not only fled from. The person lists ways out, weighs each by cost in time and distance, how likely it is to work from what they have seen, their courage and fear, and picks one: keep a fire burning at camp (wood trip then fire), drive it off with a party, or first make a spear. They reckon it in their journal and say it when asked what they are planning.

**Team plans.** A party leader asks nearby armed people to come (willingness from boldness, trust and fear), a team forms with a rally point at camp, they gather, then go together for the beast. Those in the party do not run from it. At the beast the chance of bringing it down rises with each spear there; a failed attack leaves a bite (`wound`). `world.teams`, `person.team`; chronicle entries when a party is gathered and when the beast falls.

**Far-off plans.** `scoutPlan`: when nothing with wood or food is known within 55 m of home, or from a curious person's wish to know, they pick an unexplored sector at 55–110 m, check there-and-back fits in the light left (80%), and go and return (at most one trip every two days each).

**Cause and effect from experience.** New law of the world: beasts will not come within 15 m of a burning fire. People who see a beast turn from a fire count it (`person.rules.fireRepels`: hits and tries; a beast near them with no fire counts as a miss); after two sightings they work it out ("fire keeps the wolves away"), it joins their ideas, spreads by talk and books, and weighs in `solveProblem`. The belief is a ratio, so a person who has seen it fail believes it less.

**A language model as planner (optional, off by default).** `eden/llmPlan.ts`: the Voice settings get "Let it help people plan". When on and the voice is reachable (Ollama on this laptop, or a free service), about every 45 s one adult with something to work out (a beast near, or a curious mind) is asked, with a fixed vocabulary and a plain-words account of what they know (places with distances, beasts, carried goods, ideas, the light left). The reply is parsed and every step is checked against what they know, what they will be carrying at that point, the map and the clock; impossible plans are thrown away with the reason (shown in the voice status), sound ones become the person's own plan. Nothing the model says runs unchecked; the minds are unchanged when it is off. Not tested here against a real model: only the checking is tested.

**Measured.** Unit tests: a party of three with spears and no wood drives off a wolf; two sightings make a belief and a chronicle line; a scouting plan has two legs and respects daylight; seven kinds of bad model output are refused. Ten-year worlds: 54–174 plans each; a party fought in one; scouting in two. Season sweep (4,000 lives): urgent-need time 4.9%, within what a person would do.

**Still open:** plans shared among more than a pair of roles (one leader, equal members); a model that can invent new kinds of step (it can only choose from the vocabulary); real use of a model on this laptop is untested.
