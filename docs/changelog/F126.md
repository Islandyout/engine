# F126 Eden: the people are the hunter bodies

- Everyone (Adam, Eve, and each person born or brought in) now has the hunter's base body, male or female, the rig that carries the shared clip library (combat, locomotion and the Universal Animation Library 2), instead of the old clothed models that had only idle, talk, walk, run, wave, hit and death.
- They are dressed with `world.wear` from the kit's pieces (peasant or casual clothes, hair and beards, five looks for men and four for women, with different colours), chosen by who they are.
- What a person does now looks like it: eating and drinking (`ual_consume`), harvesting food (`ual_farm_harvest`), planting (`ual_farm_plant_seed`), chopping wood and building (`ual_tree_chopping_loop`), washing (`ual_farm_watering`). These loop until they do something else (`C.actionLoops`). `actionOf` now names what is done (eat, drink, harvest, plant, chop, craft, wash, fish, rest...).
- The F125 body poses (sit, sleep, mourn, warm, fish, work) work on both skeletons; the figure's facing direction is found from the ball-of-foot bone.
- Not done yet: walking while carrying (`ual_walk_carry_loop`), a real lying clip, talking with the hands.
