# F79 — GATEBREAKER M0: the manhwa look and action controls (0.79.0)

GATEBREAKER is an original hunter action game in the manhwa style, built on this engine ([GAME_DESIGN.md](../gatebreaker/GAME_DESIGN.md)). M0 is its look-and-feel test: the hunter Han Seo-jin against a goblin in a torch-lit gate room, drawn like a comic panel. Open it from **Games → GATEBREAKER**.

## For people making games

- **The Manhwa style** (PostProcessing **Style**):
  - Toon shading: light falls into three tones with hard shadow edges, highlights are damped, and the ambient light colours the unlit tone.
  - **Ink** lines where the depth jumps (silhouettes), where it bends (corners) and where flat colours meet.
  - A **rim** light on characters.
  - On the fighting side:
    - blows show as inked sound words (SHK, BOOM, CLANG, SHING);
    - heavy hits, finishers and perfect dodges get radial speed lines;
    - finishers and parries flash an inverted black-and-white impact frame.
  - Ambient occlusion is off in this style.
- **Melee**:
  - Four more move inputs: `skill1`, `skill2`, `skill3` and `ultimate`.
  - **Shadow Step**: an attack right after a perfect dodge puts you behind the attacker and plays the list's `shadow_step` move.
  - **Right hand / Left hand** hold catalog models, such as twin daggers, fitted to any Quaternius-skeleton character.
  - The lock-on camera sits over the right shoulder so the Player never hides the opponent.
  - The Player's own overhead health bar is gone (the fighter HUD shows it).
- **New models** (CC0):
  - the Hunter and the Goblin, on the combat clips' skeleton;
  - 24 KayKit dungeon pieces (category Dungeon);
  - eight Quaternius hand weapons (category Weapons): dagger, knife, two swords, claymore, spear, scythe, double axe.
- **GATEBREAKER M0** (`examples/gatebreaker/m0.json`, built by `tools/gatebreaker/build_m0.ts`):
  - **Controls** (no jump, no kick button): LMB attack, RMB heavy, Space dodge, Shift block or tap to parry, Q/E/R skills, F ultimate, Tab lock on.
  - **Twin-dagger moves**: a four-hit chain ending in a spinning kick; an armored cross-cut, or a launching rising slash out of the stabs; Viper Rush, Fang Whirl and Shadow Fang; the Thousand Fangs ultimate.
- **Fixes**:
  - **Settings entities** (UI, Environment, PostProcessing, AudioSettings, InputActions without a model) no longer get an invisible 1 m physics box. That box sat at the origin and stopped anyone walking through it.
  - **AI melee fighters** keep their model's proportions.
  - **Overhead health bars** follow the simulated position.
  - **Tab** no longer moves the browser's focus during Play.

## For people working on the engine

- `editor/manhwa.ts`:
  - the toon patch on the standard material (one shared set of uniforms, so switching style recompiles nothing);
  - `InkPass`, which reads the depth textures that `attachDepth` gives the composer's targets.
- `editor/comicFx.ts`: the lettering, speed lines and impact-frame timing.
- `editor/heldWeapons.ts`: the hand grip, measured on the rig's rest pose.
- `editor/settingsEntity.ts`: what counts as a settings-only entity.
- `MeleeButton` has nine slots. `force_move` can interrupt a playing move. `Fighter` gains `counter_window`, `counter_target` and `script_interrupt`.
- `tools/models/make_characters.mjs` builds the hunter and the goblin.
- Tests:
  - the parser's new slots;
  - a bridge Shadow Step duel;
  - an AI fighter crossing a floor slab;
  - the toon patch still matching three.js;
  - the M0 scene's layout, controls and arms.

## Limits

- **Frame rate**: 60 fps on real hardware is untested here. The cloud test machine renders in software.
- **Shared gauge**: skills and the ultimate share the energy gauge. A separate mana gauge comes later.
- **Weapons**: held weapons are visual. Hits are still spheres from the move list.
