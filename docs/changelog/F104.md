# F104 Eden: a fuller earth and deeper people

Builds on F103 (the garden, the minds, the Creator tools).

## The earth

- The sun's hours follow the year (`daylight`, `isDark` in `apps/editor/src/eden/earth.ts`): a 15-hour day at midsummer, 9 hours at midwinter. People sleep when it is dark, so winter nights are long. The sky shows a short winter day as a short day (`solarHour`).
- The moon goes round every 8 days (`moonPhase`, `moonLight`, `moonName`); the panel header names it at night.
- The river holds fish (`world.fish`, kept by `step`): they breed in the warm months, keep to the deep in winter, and a few come down from upstream, so it is never quite empty. Catching one costs patience and thins the river.

## The people

- Fishing: a hungry person who knows the river fishes when there is no easier meal. Each try takes about half a minute and may catch nothing.
- Grief and graves: when someone dies, a grave is left where they fell (a standing stone). Those who loved them carry grief that fades over days, caps how happy they can be, is eased by company, and draws them to sit by the grave. Others who are fond of them comfort them (existing behaviour). The panel shows "grieving".
- Lessons: about twice a day a person thinks back over what they lived through. A cold or hungry spell teaches them to prepare for winter earlier; repeated frights teach them to keep further from danger. The lesson is written in their journal, changes their choices, and they can say it aloud.
- Speech: they talk about fish, graves and what they have learned.

## Checks

`tests/edenDepth.test.ts` covers day length, the moon, fishing and the river's count, grave and grief and mourning, lessons, and saving all of it. The lifelike check now carries every counter in its rows (births, goals, hunting, fishing, mourning, lessons), so a large sweep can report them; before, only the survival counters crossed from the worker threads.

The lifelike check's upper bound for sleeps a day went from 1.5 to 1.6: with 14-hour winter nights people sometimes wake for a drink and sleep again, which is how people sleep when nights are that long.

Not yet: weather beyond rain and temperature (wind, fog, snow), terrain that erodes, more kinds of plant and animal, illness, seeing the moon and graves on a real GPU.
