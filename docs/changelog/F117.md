# F117 Eden: people no longer sink under the ground

- **Cause:** the two great landmark trees were built as solid models, and a solid model gets a collision box the size of its whole mesh. A 30 m oak is a box about 25 m wide, so it covered the middle of the garden and shoved Adam, Eve and anyone brought in or born there down through the terrain (a body was found 6 m below the ground, walking about under it).
- **Fix:** the landmark trees are no longer solid as a whole; each has a small trunk post at its foot (`tools/eden/build_eden.ts`). Checked in a browser: 15 points from the centre of the garden to 100 m out each leave a body about 0.9 m above the ground (before, every point within about 12 m of the centre put it 6 m under).
- A body that does end up far below the terrain (any other cause) is lifted back and the Creator is told once. For the desktop app and tests there is `window.edenDebug()` (where each mind and body is, and the ground under it) and `window.edenPut(id, x, z)`, only when the window announces itself as the desktop app.
