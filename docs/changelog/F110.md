# F110 Eden: names for places, and growing up in the same face

- The first adult to stand at a place may name it (the river becomes "Slow Water", the highlands "the Grey Crown"); the name passes to others when they talk, is said in speech, saved with the world and shown beside the place in the Minds tab (`world.names`).
- A person keeps the same face and clothes from birth to old age: the child, youth and adult bodies are the same model at three sizes (child 60%, youth 82%, adult 100%), and the body swaps as they grow (the swap itself already existed; children used to have only two looks, unlike their adult selves).
