# F114 Eden: a chronicle of the world

- A Chronicle tab in the panel lists what has mattered to everyone, newest first: births and stillbirths, deaths, customs that formed, places named, dogs that chose someone, people becoming known for something, tempers lost and quarrels made right, the elderly and very young falling ill. It is kept with the saved world (`world.chronicle`, the last 400 entries).
- The Minds tab shows each person's family: mother, father, children and partner (including those who have died).
