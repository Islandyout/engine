# F118 Eden: nobody walks into the lake

- The lake is too deep to wade into, so it is a no-go circle for people and animals (`world.noGo`, `keepOut`): the mind pushes anyone who would step into it to its edge. When a person is sent somewhere across it, the page works out a point round the near bank (`routeAround`) and the body walks to that first (the three body scripts take an optional via point in their target message).
- The river is now shallow enough to wade (0.8 m at its deepest, was 1.6 m).

Not yet: bridges and fords, swimming, boats.
