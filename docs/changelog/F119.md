# F119 Eden: walk the world as a hunter

- **"Walk the world"** (a button in the panel) puts you in the world as a hunter: the kit's ranger outfit (hood, jerkin, boots) on the same base body as the murim game's player, a spear in hand, a third-person controller. W A S D move, Shift runs, Space jumps; J (or the left mouse button) strikes with the spear in a three-part chain, K sweeps, F kicks, X rolls, R guards, G looses an arrow. The camera follows you; **"Watch from above"** parks you again and goes back to watching. The people keep living while you walk (`tools/eden/lua/hunter.lua`, entity "You" in the scene).
- The wildlife can be hunted: deer, foxes, wolves, horses, dogs and alpacas have health. A kill is told to the people's world (the creature is gone from the herd and the minds), is written in the Life log, and a deer's meat restores health (`creature.lua`, `C.edenSlain`). Prey run from you, and a wolf or fox that gets close bites.
- Models for people and creatures (and the shelter, bush and rock) are preloaded with the scene by hidden far-off entities; before, a creature showed as a grey box until its model arrived.
- The two trunk posts at the foot of the great trees are invisible blockers (they had been drawing as boxes).
- Found and fixed on the way: the page's build recipe left out the sound effects (the audio folder was in the wrong place), so the earlier installers made no footsteps or impacts.

Not yet: people reacting to you (they do not notice the hunter), dying and carrying on as a hunter beyond waking in the garden, a bow that needs arrows, carrying meat to the people, hunting by the people's own spears competing with yours.
