# F128 Eden: ask them to follow, and they turn to face you

- In the talk box: "Follow me" (or "come with me") makes someone who trusts you, or is warm by nature, walk with you for ten minutes of their time, keeping near and running to catch up; a real need (hunger, thirst, tiredness, cold) or danger calls them away and they say so. "Stay here" lets them go about their day.
- Quick buttons in the box for the common things to say (Hello, How are you?, Where is water?, Where is food?, Need help?, Follow me, Stay here, Any news?).
- Someone being talked to turns to face you.
- The wave is a real wave (elbow out, forearm up, hand moving), seen in a screenshot; the talk pose has a hand coming up as they speak.
