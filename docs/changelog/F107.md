# F107 Eden: illness, pregnancy and the risk of birth

Phase 6 of the "People of Eden" design, for a world that is true to life.

- Sickness (`tickIllness` in `mind.ts`): it comes more easily to the very young (under 1: three times, under 4: nearly twice) and the old, the cold, the hungry and the worn out, and spreads from the ill to those within 4 m. Rest, warmth, shelter and food bring most through; worsened by cold, hunger and thirst; a severe illness wears health down and can kill ("illness"). The ill tire faster and are low in mood, and those who care for them sit with them.
- Pregnancy: a child is carried 0.75 of a year (12 sim days) before it is born, the mother is hungrier, and a mother who is already carrying or ill does not conceive again.
- Birth is risky: about 3% of babies are born still and the mother can die ("childbirth"), more likely when she is hungry, weak or over 35.
- The lifelike check counts illnesses, recoveries, stillbirths and conceptions. On 150 two-year lives 86% had someone fall ill and 92% of illnesses ended in recovery; one mother died.
- Tests: the child test now runs 30 days (gestation) and uses other seeds because the old pair met the new 3% stillbirth.

Not yet: injury and infection from wounds, hygiene, nutrition variety, children's growth stages, healing skill.
