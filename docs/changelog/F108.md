# F108 Eden: customs and standing

Phase 5 of the "People of Eden" design (`apps/editor/src/eden/norms.ts`).

- Three customs can form without anyone deciding: sharing food with the hungry, nobody taking the last of the food while another goes hungry, and whoever finds the fire low feeds it. A custom grows each time it is kept (or a wrong is seen) where others can watch; alone, nothing counts. At half strength it is a custom: everyone writes it down, talks about it, and holds themselves to it (the one who would take the last meal holds back, sharers are drawn to share, people feed a low fire). Breaking it costs trust. A custom unused for two months fades.
- Standing: the average trust the others have in a person, shown in the Minds tab as respected, trusted, of no great standing or distrusted, and it shapes who is sought out.
- The Minds tab lists the customs.
- 150 two-year lives: 67% of groups formed a custom.
- The lifelike check's allowance for deaths in gentle climates went from 2% to 6% of lives, because illness and childbirth now kill (2% of lives on this run).

Not yet: customs people start themselves (a meal at dusk), names for places, rules that punish beyond trust, rules between families.
