# F74 — Turning worlds, craters and lava rifts, the landing radar, and the Talari (0.74.0)

Closes the terrain, rotation, landing and character gaps left in the [Pale Signal gap map](space/PALE_SIGNAL_GAP_MAP.md).

## Turning bodies (`engine::gameplay::space`, bridge, editor)

- **`Body::day`**: seconds per turn about the body's +y axis (0, the default, doesn't turn).
- **Frames**: ships fly in the non-rotating frame; surfaces, sites and landmarks are fixed to the body.
  - **Conversions**: `body_spin(body, t)` and `surface_velocity(body, position)`.
  - **Physics**: surface queries, touchdowns and drag convert between the two frames.
  - **The ground**: a landed ship rides the turning ground at its surface velocity; air turns with the body; touchdown and ground speed are measured relative to the ground.
  - **Placing**: `place_landed` takes body-fixed directions and the time.
- **Bridge**:
  - The site frame's origin and axes are body-fixed, so `to_local`/`from_local` include the turn. Re-anchoring, sites, latitude/longitude and the walk heightfield all work on the turning body.
  - `editor_space_frame` now maps the non-rotating frame to the walk frame, and `editor_space_body_spin(i)` gives each body's turn. A planet's mesh and landmarks are drawn with the frame times its turn.
  - Path points come back body-fixed.
- **`SpaceSystem.bodies` option**: `day=<seconds>`.
- **Pale Signal**:
  - Days: Tethys turns every 1200 s (a solar day of about 969 s), Cinder 1800 s, Ossuary 2400 s, Hollow 3000 s; Vell is tidally locked.
  - Kestra starts mid-morning, and the NPC clock follows Kestra's solar time.

## Terrain features

- **Body options**:
  - `craters=<0..1>`: impact craters on two lattice scales, with bowls and raised rims;
  - `rifts=1`: lava channels along the zero lines of a broad noise field;
  - `dunes=1`: wind-aligned crests in sand basins.
- **Integration**: features are part of the terrain height, so flats (sites) still flatten them and walking, landing and scatter all see them.
- **Drawing**: `space::lava` and `editor_planet_lava` report lava under a point. Planet chunks colour cooled crust and glow in the channel through a `lava` vertex attribute and the material's emissive term.
- **Staged** with `editor_space_body_features`.
- **Pale Signal**:
  - craters on Vell, Nemesis, Cinder and Ossuary;
  - rifts on Cinder;
  - dunes on Ossuary.

## Landing polish

- **New `editor_space_value` fields**:
  - 47: ground slope under the ship;
  - 48–50: the gear's sink, slope and drift limits (sink scaled by gear condition);
  - 51: water under the ship.
- **Landing radar** (flight HUD, below 400 m):
  - a radar-altitude tape with a sink-rate bar;
  - SLOPE, SINK, DRIFT and WATER calls;
  - a SAFE / UNSAFE verdict against those limits.
- **The NAV autopilot** gets its name in the assist readout.
- **Water spray** (`ExplorerFx.splash`): a ring of droplets on a water touchdown, and spray kicked up by belly thrusters low over water.

## The Talari model

- **`assets/source/kit/people/talari.glb`** (catalog 175, animated), built by `tools/models/make_talari.py` from the CC0 Quaternius mannequin.
  - Kept: the same skeleton, skin weights and nine clips.
  - Changed: a narrower torso and a longer skull with a crest.
  - Added: a tunic and shoulder mantle skinned to the spine, with vertex-coloured hems so a Material tint reads as cloth.
- **Pale Signal**: all 24 named Talari and the passers-by use it, in the model's own proportions, with job accessories.
