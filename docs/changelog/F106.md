# F106 Eden: who people are, and what they get good at

Phases 3 and 4 of the "People of Eden" design (`apps/editor/src/eden/identity.ts`).

- Every person has values (kin, fairness, freedom, tradition, comfort), talents (how fast they learn five skills) and a short life story. Children take after both parents with some chance, and their story names their parents. The Creator can write a story: "Adam's story is ...".
- Values change choices: kin share food first with family; fairness makes the last meal taken in front of a hungry person sting more; freedom sends people exploring; comfort makes people build sooner.
- Five skills (foraging, fishing, hunting, farming, building) grow with use, faster for the gifted, fade a little with neglect, and make people better at the thing: more fish and game caught, more from each plot, a roof for less wood. People are drawn to what they are good at, so work divides itself; the best skill becomes a role ("the fisher") written in their journal.
- People teach each other when they talk: whoever is better shows the other, a parent's child faster. The Minds tab shows story, role, skills and values.

Measured on 150 two-year lives: groups where someone became known for something 100%, where someone was taught 97%, falling out and making up 91% and 81%.

Not yet: children learning by watching alone, roles that change what others ask of someone, norms and customs, illness, voice with memory context.
