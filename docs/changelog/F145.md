# F145: Eden phase H, violence, cruelty, causes, consequences and justice

`conflict.ts` (state on `Person.conflict` and `World.conflict`; hooks in `mind.ts`: `conflictStep` per person after `heartStep`, `conflictBias` after `heartBias`, `conflictWorld` once per step beside `fadeNorms`, `theftConflict` in the steal case; `persist.ts` saves the world's record). It models violence as consequences and reasons, never as graphic text. Every chance comes from a hash (as in `heart.ts`), not the person's own random stream, and every act yields to thirst and hunger (nobody starts anything at 0.6 hunger or thirst or above).

**Decision, stated plainly: sexual violence is deliberately not modelled.** There is no act, state, fact or cause for it anywhere in the engine. Simulating it would only mean writing a mechanism that produces it, with nothing useful to learn from the mechanism; what history shows about war, bondage and power is carried by the coercion, power, fear and bondage machinery below without it. **Child abuse is modelled only as harsh punishment and neglect**: a count on the child, a drop in how well they were cared for, a hardened attachment (secure to anxious to avoidant), a lapse tally and guilt on the parent. Nothing is described.

**H1.** A fight wounds both, the weaker more, and a bystander who is fair and bold blunts it. A killing (or a murder, which is meant beforehand and kept secret if it can be) is a fact, makes the dead person's kin and close friends hold a grudge against the killer, puts guilt on the killer, and makes those who saw it afraid. A grudge turns into revenge when the holder is near, bold, and the feud has not worn them out; law (a custom of fairness plus cases accepted) holds the avenger back. Blows between houses are counted as a feud; a feud nobody feeds for twelve days wears out. Bands (`splitBand`; strangers count as another band for forty days) raid each other under want, prejudice and propaganda; a raid takes food, wounds defenders, rarely kills; a war opens on the first raid and ends in exhaustion with tribute, or weariness. Execution is only for a killing, only under strong law, and the doer's kin may turn on the judge unless the law is strong.

**H2.** Bullying (the strong, bold and unfair pick on the weak; onlookers challenge or join); coercion of food from the weak in want; bondage after three coercions with no one to stand up for the victim (a daily tithe in food, never taken from the starving; freed by friends, by law, or by escape); harsh punishment and neglect of children with after-effects on attachment.

**H3.** Hoarding from the store, cheating in a swap (may be found), fraud on the favour ledger (found when the books are compared), bribery of a judge (refused by the honest, taken by the dishonest, may come out and cost the judge their name), betrayal of an ally (counts in the ally's heart record so love turns bitter), informing (costs the informer with the doer's kin), scapegoating of an outsider or the little-liked after a death, mobs of three or more (more harm than one, less guilt each), tribalism (bias to one's own band, away from a feared one), prejudice (grows with want and fear, falls with friendship, makes judges harder), propaganda (a leader moves opinion in proportion to trust).

**H4.** Every act is logged in `world.conflict.log` with a cause from a fixed list (scarcity, fear, status, honour, guarding, loyalty, desperation, power, revenge, greed, anger) and a reason; `causesOf(world)` counts them. A camp in want commits more fights, coercions and hoarding than the same camp in plenty (tested). `tenderAndCruel(person)` is true of someone who has given freely and done harm.

**H5.** An ethos follows what the people hold dear (honour: revenge expected; shame: standing falls further when seen; guilt: the conscience bites harder). A case goes before the most respected adult who is not kin to either side: warning, restitution (or a debt paid when they can), jail (only with law, a day or two, released to eat and drink), exile (they walk away for days; the others keep away), execution. A dishonest judge can be bribed; a judge is lenient to friends and harsh on those from a feared band. Forgiveness (a sorry doer, a patient wronged) and reconciliation end a feud; restitution accepted ends it too and the test shows no further blows for a week. Guilt fades over about a year of sim time, not days.

**Not done, said plainly.** Sexual violence (by decision, above). War is abstract: raids are resolved by comparing strength, nobody marches, and war needs two bands (nothing makes two bands in a default world until Phase G/I; strangers are the only second group). No prisons as buildings, no courts beyond one judge, no laws written or customs of punishment that differ between groups, no pardons by the leader, no lifelong grief for the killed beyond the existing grief, no lynching beyond a rare mob death, no inheritance of vengeance across generations beyond kin grudges, no weapon beyond the spear, no animals fought for status, no use in the UI, no model-written text. Acts are instant and local (within 8 m) rather than walked to. Facts about conflict are not saved (`world.facts` was never saved). Nothing here has been seen in the running game.
