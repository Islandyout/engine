# F146: Eden phase I, the wider world, the body and the Walker's body (simulation parts)

Two new modules, `apps/editor/src/eden/world2.ts` and `body.ts`, hooked into `mind.ts` in a few places (imports at one spot; `world2Step` after the regrowth loop, `bodyStep` and `bodyBias` beside the heart's, `bodySpeed` and `sightMul` in walking and noticing, `livestockOptions` and the `tame` case, `birthComplication` in `giveBirth`, `fertilityOf` in conception). `persist.ts` saves `world2`; the Walker's body is saved inside `world.walker`.

**I1 other settlements.** `enableWorld2(world, seed, n)` adds settlements with a population, a land that wears, a temper and an attitude to the camp. Each sim day they grow or starve, send trading parties (food out, hides and seed in, clothing, friendlier), raids (driven off by strong armed defenders, or the stores are taken and someone may die; a gift ends the war) and migrants (they arrive as strangers for the people to judge). Home people who are able leave a spent land for a better settlement (never while thirst or hunger is pressing). Settlements collapse and are resettled. Chances come from `world2.rng`, not a person's stream. Off until enabled; the page enables it.

**I2.** Geography fixed by the seed (mountains, a river, a sea) lengthens journeys or blocks them (`travelDays`). Long climate: two slow cycles change the climate the weather is drawn from, with a chronicle line when the era turns. Epidemics start in a crowded settlement, travel with trading parties and migrants and between near settlements, sicken home people, and leave the survivors immune (`body.immune`). Resource depletion: too many people for `landCapacity` wear the soil, which slows regrowth; a spent land is announced and sends people away. Fire: `fireDanger` from heat, days without rain and wind; an unattended campfire can escape in a dry spell, lightning can strike near the camp, a fire can burn the shelter, rain puts it out. Domestic animals: cow, alpaca, donkey and horse are won with food through the existing `tame` intent; owned cows give milk, alpacas wool, donkeys haul wood, and all keep near home and go free when the owner dies.

**I3 body.** Growth curve for height by sex and genes, stunted by childhood hunger, shrinking in old age; strength from size, fitness and age; fitness built by walking and work; sleep debt (slows, sours the mood, bias to sleep that yields to thirst and hunger, and a microsleep); sight and hearing age; congenital and acquired disabilities (limp, poor sight, poor hearing, weak) with effects on pace, sight and what is wanted; lasting scars; lice from long filth; pregnancy loss and pre-eclampsia; birth complications (haemorrhage, obstructed labour, childbed fever) wired into `giveBirth`; a sickness of the bed that passes between partners and lowers the chance of a child.

**I5 Walker.** `world.walker.body`: look (height, build, skin, hair, tunic) editable with `customiseWalker` (a bad value is refused with its reason), hunger, thirst, tiredness and dirt that rise as you walk, eat and drink from the pack, pace that falls with them (the Creator is never killed, only slowed), age that advances with the world, scars from hard hurts, and `walkerDeed`: striking, stealing, giving and healing change a person's trust and fear, and nearby witnesses' trust.

**Not done, said plainly.**
- **I4 presentation is not done at all.** No faces, blinks, lying-down clip, swimming, rowing, extra music, lighting, wear or dirt rendering, or editable-look UI, and no screenshot of any of them. Nothing in this change was seen in the running game.
- The host is wired only for: enabling the wider world, ticking the Walker's needs while you walk (and eating or drinking from the pack), scarring the Walker when a danger hurts, and keeping the Walker's body in saves. The Walker's look is not drawn on the avatar, the Walker's pace is not applied to the avatar's movement, there is no UI to customise or to see needs, and the host never calls `walkerDeed` (it has no way yet to strike or heal a person).
- Settlements are numbers, not places in the scene: nothing is drawn for them, trading parties and raiders are not seen arriving, and geography changes only journey times and settlement capacity, not the terrain. Wars are raids on the camp only; settlements do not fight each other and the camp does not raid or trade outward. No leaders or councils decide any of it.
- Migrants and emigrants pass the edge of the land as in `strangersStep`; emigrants do not come back or appear in their new home as people.
- Fire does not spread across plants and no one fights it; people only flee and the shelter may burn. Fire things use the campfire model.
- Resource depletion is a soil and game number worked from head count and hunting, not tracked per tree or field. Game stock is kept but does not yet change how many animals spawn.
- Horses have no use beyond being owned; milk, wool and hauling are daily tallies, not acts people are seen doing.
- Menarche and menopause were already in lifecycle.ts and are not changed. Hygiene beyond the number is only lice. Sexual health is one sickness with no treatment other than time and medicine knowledge.
- Weight and nutrition are not modelled, only height and strength.
