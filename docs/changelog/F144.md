# F144: Eden phase G, society

`society.ts` (state in `world.society`, plus `group`, `rank`, `rites` on a person; saved with the world). One call per world step (`societyStep`), one in the choice scorer (`societyBias`), one after a theft (`onTheft`); chances come from a hash, not the person's random stream. **Groups** hold a people's leader, council, laws, taboos, priest, customs, words and camp; everyone starts in "main". **Leaders:** status is the others' trust, best skill, age, faith and inherited rank; the highest leads (a rival must beat them by 0.1), the next three are the council. When a leader dies the heir is their eldest adult child (rank passes at four-fifths from parent to child), else the council's first. **Laws:** a custom of strength 0.65 or more that a majority of leader and council vote for (a very free-minded voter says no) becomes law. A theft where a law covers it is a case; after a delay a judge (leader unless a party or of a party's house, then a councillor) weighs witnesses and `judge()` and rules guilty (repay food or owe a favour), pardoned (the thief was starving) or dismissed. **Taboo:** a priest declares, after two feasts, that no one works on a feast day; evening work options lose score (never when thirsty or hungry). **Rites:** naming at half a year, coming of age at 4, a wedding when a pair forms, a funeral for each death (mourners within 40 m are eased, more with a priest), each in the group's custom variant and needing two others present. **Festivals:** the first day of each season, kept in the evening at the fire or camp; people are drawn there, joy is recorded, and the priest's sermon passes their answers to those who hold none. **Feuds:** between houses (patrilineal line); thefts feed them, they are inherited by everyone born into either house (affinity falls), cool over years, and a marriage across the line eases them; allies of the wronged are drawn in. **Alliances:** two houses with a marriage or three warm friendships and no feud. **Religion:** a priest (the most faithful and trusted), a schism when one question has two answers each held by two or more (each side gets a priest, the sides cool to each other). **Splitting:** with six or more adults, a schism or feud that has heated up sends the smaller side to a camp of their own; founders each recall each custom (65% true), the majority becoming the new people's way (founder effect); every season each people's customs or words may change, and `divergence()` measures how far two peoples differ.

**Not done, said plainly.** No other settlements, trade or war with them, nor migration beyond the split (Phase I). The leader has little power: no orders, taxes, or followers acting on their word; the council votes only on laws. Only theft can be judged; no other law is enforced, no punishments beyond repayment, owing and shame (banishment and violence wait for Phase H). The only taboo is working on a feast day; there is no taboo from a harm, no incest taboo of its own (the old `forbidden` check stands). Feuds start only from thefts and ignored rulings, not from affairs or violence. Dialect words are tracked and drift but speech does not use them; festival names and customs show in the chronicle only. Festivals are not shown in the game's UI beyond the chronicle. A new group's camp is a point on a circle, not a built place. Priests have no conflicts beyond the schism and no powers beyond sermons and consolation at funerals. Not yet seen running in the game.
