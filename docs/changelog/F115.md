# F115 Eden: what one knows passes to another

When two people talk, one tells the other about a place the listener has never seen: water, food, a roof, something to fear (in that order of worth). The listener remembers it, with a softened version of the speaker's feeling about it, and can plan to go there; both journals say so, and the lifelike check counts the tips shared. Together with expeditions and place names this is how knowledge of the 400 m valley moves through a family.
