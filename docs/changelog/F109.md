# F109 Eden: a voice that knows them

Phase 7 of the "People of Eden" design. The free voice (Ollama or any free OpenAI-compatible endpoint) still only rewords what a person would say; what changes is what it knows and what it may ask for.

- `apps/editor/src/eden/voiceContext.ts` writes notes for the voice from the mind: who they are, their story, what they care about, their role, how they feel now (needs, emotions, grief, illness), the few things that mattered most lately, what they think of the listener and where the listener stands, the customs, the weather and hour, and their last lines so they are not repeated. The voice is told to use only those notes.
- It may end a line with `[offer]`, `[warn]` or `[promise]`. Each is checked before anything happens (`applySpokenIntent`): an offer needs food in hand and someone hungry (the speaker then goes and shares), a warning needs a danger they know of, a promise needs someone hungry to make it to. A promise kept raises trust, one let lapse after a quarter of a day costs trust and shame. Anything that does not hold is simply not done.
- Without a voice, nothing changes: the plain speech and the mind's own sharing stay.

Not yet: a real model's answers have not been tried here (only a mock endpoint); the tags are only as reliable as the model that writes them.
