# F73 — The whole Pale Signal expedition, the Games library, and CC0 ship and sci-fi models (0.73.0)

Works through every phase of the [gap map](space/PALE_SIGNAL_GAP_MAP.md) against the [Pale Signal](https://github.com/Islandyout/pale-signal) prototype. Engine work is general (any game can use it); Pale Signal itself is content: [PALE_SIGNAL_SLICE.md](space/PALE_SIGNAL_SLICE.md).

## Games library

- **`games/`**: one folder per game, each with a `game.json` (name, genre, description, scene path, accent). The built-in games are LAST SIGNAL, HIGH HEAT and Pale Signal. Put your own in `games/my-games/<name>/`; `games/README.md` explains how.
- **`tools/build_games.mjs`**, run by `tools/build_editor.sh`, does three things:
  - publishes every game as `<folder>.html`;
  - copies its scene to `games/<folder>.json`;
  - writes `games.json`.
- **The editor's Games button** opens the library (`gamesLibrary.ts`) with three sections:
  - Engine games;
  - My games (from `games/my-games/`);
  - In this browser: scenes saved from the editor, kept in localStorage, which you can edit, download (with a ready `game.json`) or delete.
  - Each card has Play (opens the page) and Edit (loads the scene).

## Assets

- **Models**: 11 spaceships and 26 sci-fi props, columns, walls, doors and tiles from Quaternius' Ultimate Spaceships and Modular Sci-Fi packs (CC0), taken from their GitHub ports.
- **Conversion**: `tools/blend_to_vertex_color_glb.py` converts the `.blend` sources with Blender's Python module, baking each face's palette colour into vertex colours (the catalog is textureless).
- **Catalog**: categories `spaceships` (ids 164–174) and `scifi` (138–163). Provenance is in `assets/CREDITS.md`.
- **Fix**: `tools/import_model.mjs` inserted a new category's first entry into `retiredCatalogIds`; it now inserts into `modelCatalog`.

## Engine: spaceflight (`engine::gameplay::space`)

- **Ship components**: `ShipState::engine`, `rcs_condition`, `gear` and `scanner` (0–100), on the efficiency curve `component_efficiency` (1 → 0.35).
  - Engine condition scales thrust; RCS condition scales attitude authority; gear condition scales landing tolerance.
  - Wear: rough or firm landings wear the gear, overheating wears the engine, hard turns at speed in thick air wear the RCS.
- **Wind**: `ShipInput::wind`. Drag, lift and heating use the air-relative velocity.
- **The NAV autopilot**: `Assist::autopilot` and `autopilot_command`. It climbs clear of the current body's air, then burns toward the target at a speed it can still stop from, coasts and brakes, arriving slow above the target's atmosphere.
- **Route plans**: `plan_route` estimates distance, closing speed, Δv and fuel, with a status of OK, MARGINAL or INSUFFICIENT.
- **Hidden bodies**: `Body::hidden`.

## Engine: bridge and Lua

- **Several sites per scene**: a `Site` component (name, body, latitude, longitude, radius) whose direct children are authored around its own surface point.
  - Landing within the radius anchors the frame there (`editor_space_site`, `editor_space_member`).
  - Every entity outside the active site is parked: non-solid, pinned in place, and hidden by the editor.
  - Events `site:<name>`; `space.state().site`; `editor_space_value` 40.
- **`Routine`**: "hour x z" stops on the scene clock (`world.set_clock`). The entity walks to the latest stop due.
- **`Wildlife`**: calm, wary or fleeing from the walking player (a sprint doubles the radius) and from a running ship nearby, with a home leash. It overrides the AI wander; `editor_wildlife_state`.
- **Space operations** through the new generic `space.call(op, text, …)`:
  - flight: `plan`, `autopilot`, `reserve` (once, under 35% fuel, 8.5% of the tank);
  - ship: `part` (repair or damage a component), `wind`;
  - world: `bodies` (the visible ones), `reveal`;
  - input: `board_key` (scripts can take over E beside the ship).
  - `space.body` adds closing speed, the hidden flag and the sea level.
  - `space.state()` adds component condition, autopilot and its phase, the reserve, and the site.
- **`host.send(kind, text)`**: messages from scripts to the host application, for presentation the engine doesn't own (below).
- **`editor_push`**: lets the editor's solid scatter push the walker out.

## Editor

- **Maps** (`explorerHud.ts`):
  - the system map;
  - the current world's surface map, with known sites, landmarks, waypoints and you;
  - a flight minimap;
  - an on-foot minimap with catalogued resources, wildlife, people, markers and rim arrows.
  - Latitude/longitude waypoints work across frames.
- **Weather and effects** (`explorerFx.ts`): wind-slanted rain streaks, weather fog, dust motes, footprints on soft ground, and the ship settling on its gear at touchdown.
- **Ambience** (`ambience.ts`): wind, rain, settlement, wildlife and signal layers; good, warn, bad, anomaly, discovery and thump cues.
- **Scanner**:
  - Survey Optics range and speed, weather interference and scanner condition shape progress and a confidence readout;
  - fleeing targets lose the scan;
  - looking up at open sky samples the air (`skyKey`);
  - `on_ui("scan_confidence")` precedes each scan.
- **Harvesting and prospecting**: E (through the script) removes the nearest catalogued specimen from the scatter and reports `on_ui("harvested")`; `on_ui("near_harvest")` names what's within reach. Prospecting marks the nearest deposit of the asked species.
- **Host messages**:
  - maps: `map`, `map_site`, `waypoint`, `minimap`;
  - weather and surroundings: `weather`, `dust`, `soft`, `audio`, `cue`;
  - scanning and gathering: `catalogued`, `harvest`, `scanner`, `sky_scan`, `prospect`;
  - player: `announce`, `settings`.
- **Player settings** (`playerSettings.ts`, F10 or a script):
  - quality presets (pixel ratio, shadows, scatter density), look sensitivity, invert Y;
  - reduced motion, minimap, footprints;
  - touch controls (a stick and buttons that press real keys, automatic on touch screens);
  - a 60-second frame-time capture to `profile.json`.
  - A screen-reader live region reads out banners and objectives.
- **UI text**:
  - wraps to its width;
  - breaks at `\n`;
  - is drawn in its authored colour (it was always white).
- **Spawned entities**: a `Scannable` on a spawned prefab is scannable.
- **Scatter**: keeps clear of the active site's solid structures.
- **Space view**:
  - landmark kinds (`kind=` in `SpaceSystem.landmarks`: array, monolith, ruin, camp, beacon);
  - the Pale Signal's grammar, concentric rings breathing in step around every structure;
  - hidden and `unlit` bodies.

## Pale Signal (content)

All six bodies, all 22 species, 13 sites (5 as `Site` entities), all 24 NPCs on routines, the Clade resonators, all 16 evidence items, the 3 investigations, reputation, artifact ethics, workshop actions, all 9 landmarks with the 7 fragments, hidden Nemesis and the ending.

The director also covers:
- the resource economy;
- the suit with hazards;
- atmosphere verification and the helmet;
- the system board, autopilot and storms;
- one general menu panel for the journal, upgrades, ship services, culture record, academy and controls;
- 11 lessons;
- save and load.

Data: `tools/space/pale_data.ts`.
