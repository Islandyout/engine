# F80 — GATEBREAKER M1: the E-rank Gate (0.80.0)

GATEBREAKER's vertical slice ([GAME_DESIGN.md §6](../gatebreaker/GAME_DESIGN.md)): one E-rank Gate, start to finish. Open it from **Games → GATEBREAKER**. The scene is `examples/gatebreaker/e-rank-gate.json`, built by `tools/gatebreaker/build_gate.ts` and directed by `tools/gatebreaker/lua/director.lua`.

## The Gate

1. **The prologue**: four comic panels over a night plaza in Seoul, where the Double Gate glows. Then the Ledger awakens in a system window. Space or Enter skips the panels.
2. **The tutorial**, taught step by step against a training construct: move, attack, Heavy, dodge, Perfect Dodge into Shadow Step, and parry. Each step waits until you do it; G skips the tutorial.
3. **Three goblin rooms** that seal behind you and open when they're cleared:
   - grunts;
   - archers, which keep their distance and shoot;
   - a shield-bearer, whose guard holds against light attacks until a Heavy breaks it.
4. **The Goblin Chieftain**:
   - a boss bar with its stagger bar;
   - red unblockable slams with a ground warning;
   - a Break when the stagger bar fills;
   - phase 2 below half health.
5. **The rewards**: Gate cleared, then a level-up with 5 stat points to spend (1–5, Backspace, Enter). Then the first skill, Shadow Step Dash, unlocks on Q.

The Ledger won't let you die in an E-rank Gate: below a quarter of your health it restores you and reminds you how to read the tells.

## For people making games

- **Melee**:
  - **Stagger bar and Break**: hits fill the bar; when full, the fighter is floored for the Break time and takes 1.5× damage.
  - **Guard-breakers and red attacks**: `guardbreak` breaks a block outright; neither a guard-breaker nor an `unblockable` attack can be parried.
  - **The ultimate gauge**: parries fill it.
  - **Mana**: a mana pool that refills. Skills spend it with `mana=`.
  - **Locked moves**: `locked` keeps a move unusable until `melee.unlock(name)`.
  - **Ranged fighters**: an AI with a *Ranged distance* keeps that distance and shoots. It backs off at under half speed, so closing in catches it.
  - **Shield-bearers**: a *Shield-bearer* AI keeps its guard up.
  - **Melee component fields**: Stagger bar, Break time, Mana, Mana per second, Ranged distance, Shield-bearer.
- **Reading the fight**:
  - an enemy's weapon glints just before its blow;
  - red attacks flash a red "!";
  - area attacks fill a red circle on the ground.
  - A Break gets its own impact frame and BREAK lettering.
  - A perfect dodge slows time for 1.5 s.
- **The fighter HUD**:
  - health, mana, the ultimate gauge and guard;
  - Q/E/R/F skill slots with cooldown sweeps;
  - locked skills show a padlock.
- **Scripts**:
  - `hud.system(title, body)` and `hud.system_close()`: the Ledger's system window.
  - `hud.panels(spec)`: a comic-panel cutscene, one shot per line: `x y z > tx ty tz | caption | sound word | seconds`.
  - `hud.boss(name, title)`: a boss bar.
  - `melee.unlock(name)` and `melee.stagger()`.
- **New models**: the Quaternius bow and round shield (CC0).
- **Fixes**:
  - **Invisible helpers**: entities with no visible model and nothing physical (lights, particles, cameras, director scripts) no longer get a physics box. They used to fall to the ground as invisible 1 m boxes that blocked anyone walking into them, and their stand-in box no longer shows during Play.
  - **Key names in scripts**: `input.down` and `input.pressed` accept a key's code (`KeyG`, `Enter`, `Space`, `Digit1`), as the script reference says, as well as its lowercase name (`g`, `enter`). Before, only the lowercase name worked.
  - **Scripts on helpers**: a Script now runs on any entity, including ones with no physics body, such as an invisible director or a child.
  - **Health bars**: overhead bars only show for fighters within 14 m in third person.
  - **Feet through the floor**: fighters' feet no longer sink into the floor.
    - A fighter's lowest foot is now kept on the ground its collider stands on, every frame.
    - Combat clips authored on another rig carried that rig's hip height, which pushed shorter legs (the hunter's, the goblins') through the floor.
    - The dungeon floor tiles also sat 5 cm above the ground. In M0 and the Gate they now sit 1 cm above it.

## For people working on the engine

- **`engine::gameplay` melee**:
  - `MoveDef`: `mana`, `stagger`, `guardbreak` and `locked`.
  - `FighterSettings`: poise, break time, break bonus, mana and parry gain.
  - `FighterState`: mana, stagger, Break, and the locked flags.
  - `take_hit` returns true on a Break.
  - `BrainSettings`: `range` and `shield`.
- **Bridge**:
  - `editor_set_melee_extra`;
  - a `broken` melee event;
  - fighter fields: mana, stagger, broken, windup, red, aoe and the four skill slots.
- **Editor**:
  - `editor/ledger.ts`: system windows, the panel player and the boss bar;
  - combatView's telegraphs, skill slots and foot planting (`CombatView.plant`, after the mixers);
  - `editor/settingsEntity.ts`'s rule for non-physical helpers.
- **`engine::script`**: scripts run on every entity with a Box and a Script, not only those with a RigidBody. A bodiless entity's `self` has its position and no velocity.
- **Tests**:
  - the melee unit tests cover mana, locks, guard-breakers, red attacks, parry gain, Breaks and the ranged and shield brains;
  - a bridge test covers `melee.unlock` and the Break event;
  - `tests/browser/gatebreaker_gate.cjs` plays the whole Gate, from the panels to the unlocked skill, with no errors.

## Limits

- **Frame rate**: 60 fps on real hardware is still unmeasured here; the test machine renders in software.
- **Stats**: the stat points are shown but don't change the hunter yet. Saves, and stats that matter, are M2.
- **Prologue art**: the prologue panels are in-engine shots, not drawn art.
