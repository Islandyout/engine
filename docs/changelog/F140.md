# F140: Eden phase C, a mind of one's own and a model of other minds

`mindmodel.ts`. **Self:** esteem (falls faster than it rises, drifts toward how others regard them and how capable they are), self-image (bold, kind, capable, honest, liked), willpower (spent on holding back, restored by rest and food). **Learning from experience:** each activity records how it turned out (mood, needs, health before and after) by activity, time of day and season; lessons are derived from that record (`deriveLessons`) and tilt later choices in the same setting (`lessonBias`). They replace nothing in `reflect()`; they sit beside it. **Other minds:** deeds become facts; those within 15 m know first-hand, others only when told; each person keeps a model of who else knows what. **Talk:** facts pass by gossip (more where there is trust), secrets are kept from the untrusted and some leak, accusations are met by confession (conscience and guilt) or denial; a denial fails against an eyewitness and may or may not work against hearsay; flattery and persuasion by what the other lacks; a weak bold person may bluff. **Conflict:** when two strong pulls of different kinds (duty, desire, fear, pride, need, loyalty) are within 0.12, the person hesitates (logged, cortisol), then willpower or nerve settles it with a stated reason; giving in is felt as having done wrong. Low and depressed people sometimes do nothing they could have done. Guilt is explained away, more by those with a looser conscience. **Judgement:** the same deed is weighed lighter for kin and friends, heavier for foes and strangers, with the reason; blaming a deed then doing it is hypocrisy (shame, lower self-opinion). Stealing is weighed against the eyes nearby.

**Not done, said plainly.** C2 is partial: facts and the model of who knows what exist and are used for gossip, accusation and secrets, but actions are not yet planned around a false belief the other holds (a Sally-Anne case). Surprise gifts exist as a function (`surpriseGift`) but are not yet called from the gift code. `judge` is used by accusation only, not yet by the norms or punishment code.
