# Azure Cloud Sect

An open-world murim (wuxia cultivation) game slice built on the engine: `games/my-games/azure-cloud-sect`, generated by `cd apps/editor && npm run murim` from `tools/murim/`.

## What is in it

- **World**: a mountain valley (240 m), a sect compound (paifang gate, Great Hall, pavilions, moon gate, training ground), a bandit camp, a shrine with a boss, deer.
- **Cultivation** (`tools/murim/lua/player.lua`): qi from meditation (M), kills and elixirs (H); four realms (Mortal, Qi Gathering, Foundation, Golden Core); a breakthrough (B) that needs a Breakthrough Pill and three quiet seconds, and a qi deviation if you are hurt; each realm raises damage, speed, crit, health and mana and unlocks a qi art (keys 1-4); the flying sword (V) from Foundation.
- **Combat**: the engine's Melee component with a Custom move list (martial arts plus four qi arts), lock-on, blocks and dodges.
- **UI**: the UI kit's own widgets in the Minimal style (see [UI_KIT.md](UI_KIT.md)).
- **Art**: the wuxia kit (below), the engine's CC0 character and nature kits, and tinted gear.

## The wuxia kit

`node tools/murim/assets/make_wuxia_kit.mjs [out_dir]` writes 15 low-poly GLBs: a hall, two stackable pagoda tiers and a spire (roofs are lofted surfaces that sweep up at the corners and alternate tile courses), a paifang gate, a pavilion, stone and paper lanterns, a garden wall, a moon gate, an arched bridge, a wooden training dummy, a weapon rack, an incense burner and a stele. They are original work (see [assets/CREDITS.md](../assets/CREDITS.md)), so there is no licence to carry. They were added to the catalog (category `wuxia`, ids 313-327) with `tools/import_model.mjs`; the importer names a file from its display name, so the wall, bridge and dummy are `garden-wall.glb`, `arched-stone-bridge.glb` and `wooden-training-dummy.glb`.

Why generated rather than downloaded: with the sandbox's network opened, free sources were searched. Quaternius and Kenney have excellent CC0 packs but no East Asian architecture. OpenGameArt has a few CC0 pieces (a katana, a butterfly sword, a dragon, a gong) but they are `.blend` or FBX files that need Blender, and its "temple" kit is desert sandstone. Poly Haven's CC0 models are photoreal scans (Chinese furniture, an antique katana) and too heavy for this kit's low-poly look.

## Performance

The target is 60 fps on an ordinary GPU at all times. The build machine here draws in software, so frame rate cannot be measured; what can be measured is what the GPU is asked to draw (the editor's Stats overlay: triangles and draw calls, and the C++ and script time per tick).

- **Found**: the first version drew about **5.2 million triangles** and 345 draw calls in Play. The scatter's trees came from the engine's kit and cost 8,000 triangles each, about 430 of them.
- **Changed**: low-poly trees in the wuxia kit (168 to 600 triangles), fewer bushes, models exported as one primitive with vertex colours (one draw call per model, and the static batcher can merge them), no lights on the spirit veins (each light costs every fragment in a forward renderer), one shrine light, less bloom, the HUD readouts rebuilt ten times a second instead of every tick.
- **Now**: about **0.96 million triangles** (including the shadow pass) and 299 draw calls in Play. `tests/browser/murim_perf.cjs` fails above 1.5 million triangles or 450 draw calls.
- **Already in the engine**: the frame governor lowers resolution and shadows when frames run long, and the static batcher merges still objects. The C++ simulation costs about 1 ms per tick.
- **Not measured**: the real frame time on a GPU, and the cost of the 2D canvas HUD, which redraws every widget every frame.

## Ground-up assessment

An honest read of the slice as it stood after the first build, against what is known about these games. Marks: **weak**, **partial**, **good**.

| Area | State | What is wrong | Target |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| World and level design | partial (was weak) | Was one valley with three flat pads and a road. Now Heaven Peak and two crags as landmarks, a roadside shrine, a ruined watchtower, a hermit's hut and four spirit veins. Terrain still has no water, cliffs or caves | Terrain that hides and reveals, a river, a cave, a village |
| Architecture and props | partial (was weak: boxes) | Now real pagoda forms; tents and camp are still plain boxes | Camp, village, farms in the same kit |
| Characters | weak | One mannequin and one hunter model; bandits are four copies; no martial-arts clothing | Outfits from the gear parts, enemy archetypes with different weapons and tells |
| Combat feel | partial | The engine has hit-stop, sparks, shake, knockback. Missing: enemy hit flash, per-enemy telegraphs, a fair first fight | Tune to human reaction time (about 0.3 s), readable attacks, one-or-two-strike kills at the start (Ghost of Tsushima) |
| Cultivation | partial | Realm ladder, risky breakthrough and qi arts are in, which matches the genre. Missing: places that feed qi, sub-stages, a tribulation, other paths | Spirit veins in the world that raise the meditation rate; early/middle/late/peak stages; a lightning tribulation at realm changes |
| Quests and content | weak | Five linear goals, no dialogue screen, no side content, no economy | A village with talkers and a shop, side quests, rewards |
| Audio | weak | A handful of engine sounds, no music or ambience | CC0 music and ambience, per-area mood |
| UI | partial | Widgets are in; no icons, no icon inventory, no skill tree, no full map | See the reference sheets in the showcase page |
| Performance | unknown | CPU-only here: about 1 tick a second at full quality | Measure on a GPU machine |

## Sources behind the targets

- Cultivation structure: realm ladders split into early, middle, late and peak stages, with breakthroughs as the risky step and tribulation as a recurring test; qi pulled from places in the world. Seen across the genre's games and mods (for example [Dao Path](https://modrinth.com/mod/dao-path) and [Cultivation: Xianxia Leveling and Realm Progression](https://www.curseforge.com/hytale/mods/cultivation/files/8441579)). These are listings, not design analysis.
- Open-world layout: landmarks as visual anchors and the routes between them ([How Nintendo Solved Zelda's Open World Problem](https://gmtk.substack.com/p/how-nintendo-solved-zeldas-open-world), [Realism and legibility in open world level design](https://gamedeveloper.com/design/realism-and-legibility-in-open-world-level-design)). The secondary write-ups disagree on whether the talk was GDC or CEDEC, and there is no primary source for "triangle rule" density numbers.
- Combat fairness and lethality: [Ghost of Tsushima design analysis](https://www.gamedeveloper.com/game-platforms/ghost-of-tsushima-design-analysis) and [the first encounter](https://uxdesign.cc/design-analysis-the-first-encounter-in-ghost-of-tsushima-62eb54b1624b): design around a reaction time of about 0.3 s; make it feel fair.
- HUD and menus: reference screens collected with `tools/ui_refs/grab.py` (Interface In Game).
- Books worth reading (not fetched): *Game Feel* (Steve Swink), *The Art of Game Design* (Jesse Schell), *Level Up!* (Scott Rogers), *The Image of the City* (Kevin Lynch, for landmarks and nodes).

## Roadmap

1. **Done in this pass**: the wuxia kit; the sect, gate and shrine rebuilt from it; robes for the player and elder.
2. **Done**: Heaven Peak and two crags; a roadside shrine, a ruined watchtower, a hermit's hut; four spirit veins that triple meditation speed nearby. Still to do: water, a cave.
3. **Done**: five bandits of four kinds (brawler, swordsman, spearman with a long thrust and a slow armoured sweep, two qi adepts who stay back and throw qi); a white hit flash on any struck fighter (the Impact flashes setting scales it). Still to do: telegraph cues on the sweep, a fair-first-fight pass with playtesting.
4. Cultivation: stages inside each realm; a tribulation set piece at a breakthrough.
5. Content: a village with a shop and a dialogue, two side quests, loot.
6. Audio: CC0 music and ambience.
7. UI: an item and skill icon system, the icon inventory grid, a skill tree, a full map.

## Regenerating

```sh
cd apps/editor && npm run murim            # the game
node tools/murim/assets/make_wuxia_kit.mjs # the kit (then tools/import_model.mjs to change the catalog)
```

Environment variables for testing: `MURIM_REALM`, `MURIM_QI`, `MURIM_START=x,z`, `MURIM_HP`, `MURIM_LITE=1` (no shadows or scatter, for slow machines) and `MURIM_OUT`.
